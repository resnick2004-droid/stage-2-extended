Park this idea: **$ARGUMENTS**

1. Read `PARKING_LOT.md`.
2. Append one row to its table, dated today:

   `| YYYY-MM-DD | <idea> | <where it belongs, and why it is not this repository's work now> |`

3. The third column must say **where it belongs and why it is out of scope here**, not just "later". Check it against
   `CHARTER.md`: is it a different question; prohibited (conduct tier 1 -- hedging across accounts or firms,
   simulator-fill exploitation, maximum size into major news, copying a trade across accounts, concealing a pattern --
   or automated or API order entry); a purchase or a decision that is the operator's (more history, the confirmation
   window, `docs/OPERATOR_SETUP.md`); or too rare to have power on six and a half years of NQ?
4. If the idea is actually **in** scope -- it bears on P, the guarded measures or S3c, or on a contract shape not yet
   tested -- say so instead of parking it. In a worktree, propose it in the hand-back for `TASK_QUEUE.md`; only the lead
   edits the queue, and a new item enters it under a decision row.

Parking is not rejecting. It keeps the charter's scope honest and stops the same idea being rediscovered and quietly
pursued. Promoting a parked idea needs a decision row.

Report the row you added.
