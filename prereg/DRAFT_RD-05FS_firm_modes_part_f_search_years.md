## 1. Why, and what it does to what the reading can show (E-051)

**Why.** Under E-208 every choice and every map the journey search draws on is made on the search years, and the
held-out years are read once, by the joint confirmation (E-221, O-1). The parent's part F reads the whole development
window, "the late block as late development" (CHARTER 3.6 (b) lists it), and its judged-block purposes; any lever range
drawn from its rows would carry a held-out read into the search (CHARTER 3.3 (a)). O-7 rules that part F is re-declared
on the search years; the coin side runs as pinned.

**What it does to what the reading can show.** Part F's rows describe the search years only. Each measure's starts at
its horizon h are fewer (G-5), so the headline holds fewer kept windows and more readings print "too few separated
windows for an interval"; each half (G-6) holds about two years, so most half readings at months 6 to 12 print the sign
count alone. The stability flag compares two halves of four years. The verdict arm's shift is the search years' own
(G-4). The cited `TS-N` and `LU40` rows come from the search-years deltas of RD-03 and RD-01 (D-07, D-08), so every
part-F row of the menu rests on the same years. Nothing else of part F changes: the configurations, corners, flips,
counts, uncertainty method and checks are the parent's.

  "the development window", "2020 to 2026", "2020-2026" or "the whole window" of what it reads, read the search years.
  A pinned calendar (E-029, E-050) is used only on days inside the search years.
- **G-2. The cut at the read.** Part F runs through RD-01's runner (`extended/firms.py`'s `run_plan`) as the
  search-years delta of RD-01 (`prereg/DRAFT_RD-01S_firm_contracts_search_years.md`, its G-2) cuts it: the engine's
  reads authorised on 2020-01-02 to 2023-12-29 only -- the choice block, no purpose, not ledgered -- every bar and atlas
  row after 2023-12-29 dropped at the read by a wrapper on the vendored readers, restored after the call (nothing
  vendored edited); the day blocks and dates handed to the replay and the gate ending at the search years' last
  session; any block or date after 2023-12-29 refused before any replay. **If a registered builder cannot be cut at its
  reads, the build stops and reports; this declaration does not fall back** to a whole-window build cut afterwards.
- **G-3. The cut identity, refusing** (the shared script of the four deltas, under `identity/`, the purpose `identity`,
  its own decision row; before step 3). (a) The parent's whole-window identities hold as the parent states them (G-7).
  (b) For P-14 and P-16, the stream built on the cut equals the whole-window stream's day blocks up to 2023-12-29, block
  for block, every field, the per-R outcomes included. (c) The vendored estimator of the verdict arm's constants (G-4),
  applied to the whole-window stream, reproduces the registered constants exactly. The script reads the whole window
  under the purpose `identity`, writes pass or fail lines and nothing of any cell, and is not this reading. A failure
  of (b) stops part F; of (c) alone, the verdict arm (G-4).
- **G-4. The verdict arm's constants, re-estimated on the search years.** The parent's "the bound was estimated on the
  whole development window, so an early-block reading on the verdict arm is a function of 2024-2026 through that one
  constant": here `e`, `lb`, `e_by_rr` and `lb_by_rr` are the vendored estimator's (the one the registered builder

  (G-3 (c)). The `point` arm is unchanged. If G-3 (c) fails, the verdict arm is not run: `point` is the headline, every
  row labelled so.
- **G-5. The starts and the horizon embargo** (CHARTER 3.6 (d)). The starts are every start day U1 reads inside the
  search years. Each measure is read at its horizon h (RD-03 section 5's table, carried, unchanged) only on starts with
  h sessions inside the search years: on the cut replay a start observes no session after 2023-12-29, so
  `u_select.block(records, None, h)` gives exactly these. U1's long replay of up to 252 sessions and its exact-length
  runs stop at the cut. Wherever part F says "every start" or "all starts" of what is replayed or read, read the
  search-year starts with the horizon left. By start year: 2020 to 2023, means only, not purged within the search
  years, 2023's starts reading only 2023's sessions; labelled so.
- **G-6. The blocks: the late block replaced by the search years' second half.** On the cut replay the parent's early
  block equals the headline and its late block is empty, so both are replaced, per measure at its h:
  - **all**: every search-year start with h sessions inside the search years (the headline);
  - **first**: the starts of 2020 and 2021, each read at h only if h sessions remain before the first session of 2022
    (D-621 (4)(b)'s purge, moved to the half's end);
  - **second**: the starts of 2022 and 2023, read at h only with h sessions inside the search years.
