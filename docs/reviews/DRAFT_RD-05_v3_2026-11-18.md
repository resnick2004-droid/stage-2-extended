## Readings for the pinning row

### R-1. A-18's entry test is on the order's live time, not the alert's

*Where:* A-18 (line 410) says "**no entry whose alert falls inside the window**". The governing rule, 4.7 item 3
(line 487), says "**no entry inside the window** -- decided at the alert from the clock and the calendar alone", and
13.1 (line 1076) checks "no entry inside a verified window" at zero.

*The gap:* CHARTER section 4 puts the order at least five minutes after its alert, and lets a resting order go live
then and fill at its level. So the literal A-18 does two wrong things:

- **It misses violations.** An alert in the five minutes before the window gives an order inside it (inside
  `mffu.news_tier1_rule`'s two minutes either side of a minutes release). 13.1's zero check then stops step 3 on a
  correct build, or a tier-1 entry passes if the check is also coded on the alert time.
- **It refuses harmless trades.** An alert inside the window has its order after the window ends, yet it is refused.
  This is conservative, but it is not the rule.

*The reading:* an entry's time is its order's live time: the alert plus the hand-entry latency, known at the alert from
the clock alone. A working entry order is cancelled at the window's start, as the record's rule ("no positions or orders,
limit orders included") requires. This is what 4.7 item 3 and 13.1 already say, and no rule changes. T-7 or T-4 should
hold one toy case each way: an alert just before the window, and an alert inside it whose order lands after.

### R-2. T-9's power test should meet its own stated power at each check's own cut

*Where:* 9.6 line 908: "a battery with one check shifted by eight of its standard errors breaches in every one of a
declared number of batteries (power above 0.999 at that shift)". T-9 repeats it. No number of batteries is declared.

*The arithmetic:* with N = 7,489 and a family-wise 0.01, the cuts are as follows.

| check | cut | power at 8 SE |
|---|---|---|
| one-sided Clopper-Pearson | about 4.7 SE | about 0.9995 |
| two-sided Freedman fairness check, whose cut at this scale is `sqrt(2 ln(2N / 0.01))` (the `b a / 3` term is negligible) | about 5.3 SE | about 0.996, not above 0.999 |

"Breaches in every one" of K batteries then fails on a correct build with probability `1 - 0.996^K`. For any K in the
tens, that is a material chance of stalling the build under the failure protocol, and of tempting a change of seed.

*The reading:* the text's governing requirement is "power above 0.999". So for each check type, the shift is the
smallest that gives it at that type's own cut. That is about 8.5 SE for the fairness check (9 SE is safe), and 8 for
the probability checks. K is declared with T-9 before it runs, and the assertion is a breach share no lower than the
computed power less its binomial error, not "every one".

## Notes (new material)

### N-1. Freedman's inequality is applied with the realised variance, which the inequality does not strictly cover

*Where:* 9.6, lines 873-882.

*What is right:*

- The increments `(1{target} - p) x span` are mean zero under fairness, and bounded in absolute value by the span, so
  by `b`.
- `p` and the span are fixed when the trade is entered, so the variance process `V` is predictable.
- The `2 exp(...)` form for `|S|` is the two one-sided bounds added.
- Pooling all of a run's trades in session order is a valid filtration. Within a session the trades are independent
  given the past.

*The subtlety:* Freedman bounds `P(S >= a and V <= v)` for a **fixed** `v`. Solving for `a` at the **realised** `V` is a
data-dependent `v`, which the bound does not strictly cover. The standard remedy is a union over a geometric grid of
`v` (peeling), which costs a small additive logarithmic term in the level.

*Why it is not a blocker:*

- At these scales Freedman's tail is roughly an order of magnitude more conservative than the normal tail it replaces,
  which absorbs the plug-in comfortably.
- T-9's "fair trade sequences of the coin's own law" batteries test the realised-`V` form directly.

*Fix at build:* state the plug-in form and let T-9 hold it, or implement the peeled form. T-9 already names "Freedman's
bound computed against a hand-worked case".

*On power, as the brief asks:* the cut is about 5.3 standard deviations of `S`, and `S` pools every trade of a run. So a
per-trade win-probability bias of under a percentage point is detected at the run counts of a typical cell. That is
stronger than the second text's per-account mean check, not weaker.

### N-2. `RI`'s peaks "in the same check" need a declared increment

*Where:* 9.6 line 882: "At `RI`'s funded cells the losing trades' peaks are checked the same way against their law, in
the same check."

A peak is not a win/loss indicator, so the increment the check adds is undefined.

*Fix at build:* a probability-integral increment `(F(m) - 1/2) x weight` (uniform under the law, so bounded), with its
contribution to `b` and `V`. T-8 tests it.

### N-3. The coin's stacking window counts every failure; the definition counts floor breaches

*Where:* 4.7 item 5 (line 506); 9.5 (lines 852-853).

The definition counts "at least two **floor breaches**". The coin's window state carries "the end sessions of the last
two **failed attempts**", and failures include expiry and inactivity. On the coin that overstates the exposure at
`L6`, `L10` and `L11`, which have lifespans, and on the costed coin, where stand-asides lead to inactivity.

*Fix at build:* carry only the floor failures in the window state, to match part F.

### N-4. U-32's lenient end is not the most lenient reading the record contemplates

The record's note on `mffu.<plan>.commissions` suggests both ends: "none", and the firm-wide figure. The text's lenient
end is Topstep's schedule. Topstep's schedule is below the firm-wide figure on both instruments, so the corners are
ordered correctly. But a zero-commission reading lies outside them.

*Fix:* a label beside the My Funded Futures rows: "lenient end at Topstep's schedule; zero-commission reading not run".

### N-5. A-11's "moved to Live" has one end only

The ceiling entries leave open whether "net profit" is after commissions, where the day starts, and where the
forfeited excess goes. Each is moot under A-11: the account's balance is not credited at the move, which is the
strictest reading. The firm note's Reserve on transition is not modelled (G-4).

*Fix:* the rows should say "strict: no Live credit".
