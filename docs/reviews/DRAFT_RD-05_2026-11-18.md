### B2. The flip's funded size and the coin's request are undefined where the plan has no per-request maximum, and `k_F` is ambiguous under a calendar clock

*Where:* 2.6 lines 234-236; 7 items 3-4 lines 627-632; 9.2 lines 715-718; 5.2 line 570; Appendix C row 5.

*Evidence:*

- **The flip's funded size.** `r_flip = b* / (k_F x w)`, where `b*` is "the smallest balance at which the declared
  request reaches its largest value".
- **The coin's request.** It is "the plan's own maximum ... where the record states no maximum, the reference's amount".
- **What the record says.** At `RE`, Q-136's record has `mffu.rapideod50k.payout_cap` verified as "no cap per payout
  cycle; no lifetime cap stated", and `mffu.rapideod50k.payout_request_amount` **unstated**. Its note says the plan-data
  reading "rests on plan-data fields ... not on words. A family needing it declares both ends". At `RI`,
  `mffu.rapid50k.payout_cap` is unstated. At `PRO`, `mffu.pro50k.payout_cap` is unverified.
- **Two readings of `RE`.** Under the reading "the whole profit above the buffer", the largest value is never reached,
  `b*` is infinite, and every flip trade becomes an all-in cut to the room. Under the reading "states no maximum", the
  request is the reference's `min(0.5 B, $2,000)` cut by the buffer, and `b*` is finite. The text picks neither, and
  neither reading is two-ended in 5.2 or Appendix M.
- **The ambiguity reaches every cell, not just the flips.** The coin's request decides every funded cell at `RE`,
  `RED`, `RI` and `PRO`.
- **`k_F` under a clock.** At `PRO`, `k_F` is "the fewest trading days to first-payout eligibility". Part A item 3
  computes both trading days and sessions elapsed, and the flip does not say which it uses.

*Failure allowed:* the builder chooses `b*`, the coin's request at four funded modes, and `PRO`'s flip window. These
are free parameters, which the draft says the flip does not have, and they could be chosen after a look at step 0.

*Fix:*

- Declare the coin's request at `RE`, `RED`, `RI` and `PRO` as a two-ended term of 5.2: strict, the reference amount
  cut by the buffer; lenient, the whole profit above the buffer, with the per-user cap where it is recorded. Add their
  corners to Appendix M, with the counts following.
- Define `b*` for an uncapped request, for example as the reference amount's `b*` at both ends, so that `r_flip` is
  finite and declared.
- Define `k_F` as the sessions elapsed to the first eligible close when trading every session.

### B3. The oracles of 9.6 stop step 2 on chance alone

*Where:* 9.6 lines 762-781; 14 line 963; 13.1 (b) line 928.

*Evidence:*

- **Every breach stops the step.** Each oracle "stops step 2", and the failure protocol forbids rerunning until it
  passes.
- **Too many checks for an undeclared interval.** The intervals are "its Monte Carlo interval", with neither a width
  nor a multiplicity rule. There are about 816 gross evaluation cells (pass ceiling and martingale each), 1,200 gross
  funded cells (two checks each) and up to 3,024 costed cells: several thousand checks in all. At any conventional
  per-check width, a correct engine breaches some of them.
- **Some cells sit on their bounds.** `L6` and `L11` (a static floor, a mandatory daily limit, a target twice the
  drawdown) contain cells such as `steady (1.0, r)` with `r` dividing both barriers. These cells are a gambler's ruin
  that meets `D / (D + T)` exactly, so their estimates lie above it about half the time.
- **The supremum check has no interval at all.** "No `TS-S` evaluation cell exceeds the supremum" (line 777) carries no
  interval. Stage one's README places the best static profile a small fraction of a point below the supremum, which
  is inside the binomial error `sqrt(p (1-p) / 50,000)`. Every `TS-S` flip cell's first two sessions are the profile
  `(w, 1500/w)`, and the README calls profiles of that kind "within noise" of the best. So many cells sit at the
  supremum within noise.

*Failure allowed:* a correct engine is stopped at step 2, and the reading cannot finish without breaking the failure
protocol.

*Fix:*

- Declare each oracle's width with family-wise control: one-sided where the bound is one-sided, and z set by
  Bonferroni at a stated alpha over the number of checks printed at step 0.
- Give the supremum check the same "beyond its interval".
- Keep T-9's hand-broken engines, which show the oracles still fire.

## Should-fix

### S1. The coin streams are keyed by the mode-corner, so corners are not paired and `RI` does not share `RE`'s outcomes

*Where:* 15 lines 976-978; 9.2 line 709; Appendix C row 26.

The keys are `[seed, m, w_index, r_index]`, with `m` the Appendix M index. A mode's strict and lenient corners have
different `m`, and so do `RI` (8, 9; 33, 34) and `RE` (4, 5; 29, 30). So "so `RI`'s outcome stream is `RE`'s"
(lines 709, 978) is false as declared, and so is the claim that "paired differences are ... the term's" (row 26).

*Failure:* a corner difference or an `RI`-minus-`RE` difference carries independent stream noise, yet is printed as if
it were paired.

*Fix:*

- Key by the base mode, not the corner, and give `RI` `RE`'s base index for the outcome stream.
- Say that account `i` is column `i` of each slot's draw vector over all accounts, alive or not. That is the vendored
  `RandomDraw` behaviour (`rng.random(n)` every slot), and the pairing depends on it.

### S2. `RI`'s funded flip cells fall outside the exact set

*Where:* 9.1 line 688; 9.2 lines 711-714.

The read set tests `(w + 1) r <= D` on the cell's `r`. A flip trade is sized at `r_flip`, which is cut to the room and
equals the whole drawdown at activation. So `(w + 1) r_flip > D`: the trade can be stopped by a floor its own peak
raised, which is the path law the text says it does not declare.

*Fix:* apply the test to every trade's size (`r` and `r_flip`), or print `RI`'s funded flip cells "not read".

### S3. The coin's base is stage one's engine, not Stage 2's verified contract, and two forms are missing

*Where:* 4.2 lines 348-351; 4.3 lines 380-391 (A-0); 17.3 T-1 and T-3.

  class.
- **No base comparison.** T-1 holds A-0 to propsim, and T-3 holds adapters to account classes. Nothing holds the base
  coin at `TS` to the vendored `CombineAccount` / `FundedAccount`.

*Fix:*

### S4. The costed coin's sizing during a run is undefined, and its stand-aside breaks the "inactivity 0 (checked)" claim

*Where:* 9.1 line 687; 9.2 lines 701-704; 9.3 line 723; G-9 line 420.

  said whether `n` is recomputed each trade as the room shrinks.
- **Stand-aside near the floor.** If it is recomputed, D-272's stand-aside makes the account take no trade on those

*Fix:*

- Declare `n` per trade from the room.
- Declare a stood-aside session as no trade that counts toward inactivity, and drop the "0, checked" claim on the
  costed coin.
- Charge costs inside the room (the room handed to the trade is net of the round turn and stop slippage).

- **Look-ahead.**
  - Parts A, B and E read the rules alone.
  - The flip schedule uses rule-derived constants and the trade's own stop, which is known at the alert.
  - No quantity is fitted on data.
  - The verdict arm's whole-window bound is disclosed (6 item 3).
  - Nothing reads NQ from 2026-07-17.
  - Part F's starts use U1's "h sessions left".
- **The judged-block line.** One line, three purposes, none choosing by `reader._choice_purpose`. T-0 passes.
- **The seed block.**
  - Every ref (`git grep`): only the draft on `wt/wt-scout` and its remote.
  - The main tree's working files, every worktree under `%LOCALAPPDATA%\stage2-extended-wt\`, and the runs folder:
    only the draft.
- **The counts.**
  - 13 evaluation and 15 funded modes.
  - 17 + 25 = 42 mode-corners.
  - 24 steady corners, 28 flips, 39 ladder runs, 13 gates.
  - 2,016 + 3,024 = 5,040 coin runs.
  - Each number checks against its basis.
- **The oracles' validity.** At zero edge, optional stopping with `E[pass balance] >= T` and
  `E[fail or unresolved balance] >= -D` gives `p <= D / (D + T)`. This holds through overshoot, unresolved accounts,
  locks above start, trailing floors and soft limits. The truncate barrier race makes each trade's mean exactly zero at
  a finite horizon. The funded mean gross is at most `D`.
- **`RI`'s peak law.** `P(m >= x | loss) = a (b - x) / (b (a + x))` derives from two barrier races. The condition
  `(w + 1) r <= D` is the right one for steady trades.
- **The stated-fewest check.** Topstep's 55 % gives two days, LucidFlex's 40 % gives three and its 50 % two. Each
  matches the ledger's quoted statements.
- **The quotes.** Account Stacking's fragments match `prohibited_trading_strategies` verbatim. Pro's policy quotes match
  the note.
- **Bit for bit.**
  - The vendored `simulate_combine` and `simulate_xfa` take an `rng`.
  - `RandomDraw` draws `n` uniforms every slot whatever is alive.
  - `BinaryDraw` delegates to it.
  - So "A-0 with every addition off equals the vendored function" can be tested as written, and the common random
    numbers hold across `flip` and `steady`.
- **The join's means.** Fees per pass are fees per attempt over the pass probability (Wald). Funded independence follows
  from separate seeds and a fresh start state.
- **Typed result numbers.** None found. The 1.4 GB traces to E-010's recorded peak, and "2/5" is rule arithmetic (S11).
