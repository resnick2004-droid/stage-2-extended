## B. The runner: findings, by severity

### MATERIAL

**R1. The early block reads 2024 sessions for four of its 126-session measures.**
`extended/pp_run.py:202-205` (MEASURES) with `start_frame` (178-189) and Q-17's `start_table`.

- **Evidence.**
  - Sessions and cash to the first pass, and to the first payout, are read at h = 126 (5 item 3).
  - The values are the long replay's: `first_pass`, `first_payout`, `spent_before_first_pass` and
    `spent_before_first_payout` come from U1's `start_record` over the start's whole replay, up to 252 sessions
    (`user_journey.py:147-160`).
  - The block is purged at 126 (`RD.block(recs, "early", "first_payout_sessions")`), but the value is not cut at 126.
  - Example: a start on 2023-05-01 whose first payout comes at session 200 is in the early block. Its "sessions to the
    first payout", and its cash spent before it, read sessions up to about 2024-02.
  - Q-17's own reading cuts these at the window (`user_readings.py:184-187`: `_days(..., FIRST_WINDOW)`, "among starts
    reaching it by 126"). The runner does not.
  - The runner's T-3 (`tests/pp_run_check.py:202-211`) cannot see this. Its synthetic frame builds every start's value
    "from its own h sessions only", so the horizon holds by construction.
- **What it allows.**
  - 5 item 3 and F6 say "No early reading touches a 2024 session"; for these four measures it does.
  - The early-against-late comparison, and so the stability flag, rest on a block that is not purged.
  - On all starts the four measures mix censoring levels: starts near the window's end are cut at their remaining
    sessions, the others at 252.
- **Remedy.**
  - Cut each event measure at its horizon: reached by session h or not. Days and cash count only for starts that
    reached the event by h; mark the others "not by h".
  - Print the share reaching by h on each side, as Q-17 does.
  - Make the runner's T-3 build its frame through `start_frame` from session-level toy records with events after h, so
    that a value reading past h changes the early block.

**r2. The holiday-hours reach looks one session late** (choice 4). `pp_run.py:382-387`.

- **Evidence.**
  - Arrival less lag is the request's campaign day k. That part is right: `payout_arrivals` dates an arrival at
    `activation + 1 + r + lag`.
  - But the engine's campaign day k plays historical day k - 1 of the start's window. Engine day 0 is the purchase
    day; `shared_days.py:514` and `replay_records.py:42-43` both say "calendar day = historical day + 1".

  - Requests whose cash would arrive after the window are also left out (`start_record` drops them), so the last `lag`
    sessions' requests are never counted.
- **Remedy.**
  - Use `dates[s + k - 1]`, or date requests from each account's `payout_days` plus its activation, as 8.3 itself names.
  - Test it on a toy whose requests fall on known early closes.
