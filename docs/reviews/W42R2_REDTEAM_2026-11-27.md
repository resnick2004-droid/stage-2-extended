## Blocking

### B-1 (blocking): the headroom's noise is the noise of whichever baseline came out on top, not of the design being protected

**Where.** `extended/wave42_r2.py:273-275`: `sd_best = limit_per_seed_sd(..., vals[x["best"]], ...)` is fed into
`headroom(...)`. R2.md section 4.2, lines 168-189, derives "the nominal rate" from that sd. Section 4.3, lines 195-197,
names the winner's curse only as a bias in M_best's level.

**Evidence.** Section 4.2's claim, that a design equal to the best baseline passes at Phi(z_L), needs se to be the noise
of D - M_best, where D is that design's held-out estimate. The code instead takes sd from the baseline that came out on
top in the search draw. Two cases break the claim:

- **Near-tied baselines with unequal dispersion.** When baselines are near-tied, the one that comes out on top is often
  not the true best.
- **The true best is the noisier one.** A tightly estimated tied baseline then supplies both a small se and, through
  M_worst, a floor sitting at the common value. The design equal to the true best is noisy, so it falls below that
  floor far more often than the nominal share.

Three checks show it:

1. **Gaussian abstraction.** Four baselines, independent search noise, held-out to search noise ratio set from the
   real counts (about a half at h = 252), z at L = 7. I searched 800 random configurations of true values, per-seed sds
   and correlations. Under W42-R2 the refusal of a design equal to the true best reaches about 3.7 times the nominal
   share. About one configuration in six exceeds 1.25 times nominal.
2. **End to end, through W42-R2's own `setting_rule`.** I added one scenario to `tests/w42r2_power.py`'s machinery,
   imported unchanged: four tied medians, independent draws, the first baseline eight times noisier, and the design
   given the first baseline's law. Over 100 search draws by 20 held-out draws, the design is refused at about three
   times the nominal share under W42-R2, and at about seven times under W42-R1.
3. **Equal sds, independent draws (the classic winner's curse).** The residual is small: at most about 1.2 times
   nominal, at the four-way tie. In that case `min(M_worst, ...)` protects, so the residual is acceptable if it is
   reported.

**The failure it allows.** Consider a limit on which all four baselines are close and their dispersions differ, such as
a workload or supply count, or `first_payout_days_p50` near its censoring. A design equal to the best on that limit is
refused at several times the nominal rate. E-296's stated aim (R2.md lines 25-27: "restores E-015's power claim") is
then false in a plausible H4 regime. Coin fields are keyed per baseline's own moments and starts
(`three_way_fastkeys.coin_uniforms`), so the baselines' search noises are nearly independent. That is the regime where
this happens, not the shared-draw regime the check tests.

**Remedy (a rule change, before the pin).** Replace sd_best in both terms by **sd\* = the largest per-seed sd among the
contenders**. A contender is any baseline b with M_b at or beyond M_best - z_L sqrt(se_b^2 + se_best^2). Taking the
largest of all four sds behaves almost the same.

- **Effect on the defect.** In my 800-configuration search either form brings the worst case to about 1.4 times
  nominal, and the 95th percentile below nominal.
- **Cost.** The floor goes further below M_worst only when every baseline is within the headroom. The power check
  should report that distance.
- **If the lead wants the residual winner's curse bounded at nominal.** Use z at 1 - (1 - `POWER_TARGET`) / (4 L), a
  union over the four candidate bests. Report its extra looseness beside.

Whichever form is chosen, it is declared before the pin, and both R2.md 4.2 and 4.3 restate the derivation for the
selected maximum.

### B-2 (blocking): the power check cannot fail in the regimes where the headroom binds, and it reads one limit of seven

**Where.**
- `tests/w42r2_power.py:118-138` (the scenarios), `166-171` (`_read_design`) and `231-246` (reported, no criterion).
- `tests/test_wave42_r2.py:297-322` (the nominal-rate test) and `636-649` (the reduced check).
- R2.md section 7 (lines 264-286) and section 8 (lines 308-311).

**Evidence.**

- **Equal per-unit sds.** Every scenario gives all four baselines one per-unit sd. The two-humped scenario's
  dispersions are equal as well.
- **No selection in the headroom-bound scenarios.** The only scenarios where the headroom binds (`identical` and
  `distinct_headroom`) give all four baselines one shared draw. Nothing is selected there, so the selection effect of
  B-1 cannot appear.
- **The lumpy law is tested only where the worst binds.** The two-humped scenario has `curve False` and anchor
  `worst`.
- **The unit test of the nominal rate has nothing to select from.** `test_the_old_headroom_falsely_refuses...` uses a
  single baseline and a mean statistic.
- **No declared criterion.** The full check's figures go to a hand-back. Nothing declared says which figure would stop
  the pin. The reduced test asserts outcomes over 2 x 2 draws, such as `false_refusal == 0.0` and
  `power_margin == 1.0`, which fail only grossly.
- **One limit of seven.** `_read_design` sets every other measure to zero, so E-015's joint claim is never checked.
  That claim is that a design equal to the best passes all seven limits together with at least `POWER_TARGET`, and it
  is the claim R2 says it restores.

**The failure it allows.** B-1's defect passes this check unseen. More generally, any over-refusal among the six W42-R1
limits under the new headroom is invisible. The operator's standing condition ("a strict line shows power, not only
size") is reported as met without a test that could show otherwise.

**Remedy.** Declare the acceptance criteria in R2.md section 7 before the check is run. For example, for a design equal
to the true best: refusal on each headroom-bound limit at most nominal plus three binomial se, and the joint pass of all
seven at least `POWER_TARGET` minus three binomial se. Add these scenarios:

1. Independent near-ties with unequal dispersion (the scenario in B-1, check 2).
2. Two tied at the top with two lower but still within the headroom.
3. The lumpy law where the headroom binds.
4. A lattice-valued count limit.
5. A joint read: the design's every measure drawn from the best baseline's law through W42-R1's generator, read
   against all seven values.

Each scenario must be able to fail under W42-R1's headroom or under sd_best, and it is shown failing there (a mutant
the check kills).

### B-3 (blocking): the added limit's form was chosen after a reading of H4-B, and the text says otherwise

**Where.** R2.md line 33 ("The lead pins it, before H4 is read"), lines 29-31, and section 10, lines 321-326 ("W42-R2
chooses nothing from them: the limit's key, horizon, statistic and direction are the lead's ruling").

**Evidence.**

- **What the early look showed.** E-291 records the early look (job `q213-early-look`, commit `514f6ad`) with "P-14
  beside from H4-B's held readings". The page `docs/plans/EARLY_LOOK.md` shows, for P-14's H4-B search-year readings
  and for a candidate design:
  - the exact statistic the added limit floors ("Net after twelve months, a typical user", the p50 of
    `net_month_12`);
  - its p10 alternative ("a bad case");
  - two rows that bear on W42-R1 limits whose headroom E-296 now moves (sessions to the first payout, and the first
    year's out-of-pocket peak).
- **When the ruling came.** Q-221's ruling (its row given in E-293, commit `d157661`) came after that page (`514f6ad`).
  R2.md itself says the look is why the value comes from the formula.
- **What the formula does not cover.** It removes the choice of the floor's value. It does not remove the choice of its
  form: p50 rather than p10, horizon 252 rather than 126, and a floor at all. That form was ruled after a baseline's and
  a candidate design's values on it were seen. CHARTER 2 and E-221 require the rule declared before H4 is read. Part of
  H4 (P-14's H4-B readings) had been read.

**The failure it allows.** A limit only removes designs, so this cannot raise the held-out test's false-pass rate. It
can, however, change the menu: which designs reach the finalists and which journey is reported best. For example, a
p50 floor was chosen knowing that a candidate design's typical user and P-14's sit on opposite sides of zero. Whether
that steers the menu cannot be judged from the record. That is the defect.

**Remedy.**
1. Correct line 33 and section 10. State what was read (P-14's H4-B search-year readings, the rows shown, and a
   candidate design's), by whom and when, and that the limit's form was ruled after that read.
2. A lead's row ruling whether the form stands. Give a reason for p50 and horizon 252 that does not depend on the look,
   for example MEASURES (12)'s headline statistic or CHARTER 2's "making money" term. Otherwise declare a form fixed by
   an outside rule, such as floors on both the p10 and the p50.
3. Require F-13's menu to show each finalist's limit status with and without the added floor, so the post-look limit's
   effect is visible. W42-R2 already computes the without case: `net_floor=False` and `value_w42_r1_headroom`.
4. List the read among W42-R2's prior reads as a read of H4-B, not as "figures seen".

## Minor

### m-1 (minor): the nominal rate holds at the held-out read only, not at the search-year screen R2.md says the limit is read at

R2.md lines 139-140 say the limit "is read at the search-year screen and at the held-out verdict ... at the point
estimate". The headroom is derived for J = `TEST_SEEDS` held-out seeds (section 4.2).

On the search years, a design equal to the best read at F-13's deciding size of 16 seeds has error variance
sd^2 (1/16 + 1/J_H4), on the same history. By my arithmetic, on a headroom-bound limit it is refused about a quarter of
the time, against the nominal share near three in a hundred.

This changes no R2 value, but it bears on power wherever F-13 drops cells on a limit's point estimate. **Fix:** say in
section 3.4 that the headroom's rate is the held-out read's. Hand F-13 the reading-specific form:
se = sd\* sqrt(1/J_read + 1/J_H4) on the search years. F-13's red team should treat an exclusion on a search-year limit
at 16 seeds as a power question.

### m-2 (minor): sd_best's own sampling error

R2.md line 198 declares the error unpropagated. At J_H4 = 128 the relative error of a normal-like per-seed statistic's
sd is about 6 %. In my simulation that moves the refusal rate negligibly. For quantiles of lumpy or lattice measures it
is larger. **Fix:** report a chi-square or bootstrap interval of sd\* beside each headroom-bound limit. Optionally
inflate z by sqrt(1 + z^2 / (2 (J_H4 - 1))).

### m-3 (minor): the power claim is overstated, and the citation is to a superseded clause

R2.md lines 25-27 say the correction "restores E-015's power claim". E-015 (3) reads "a limit never fails a design",
which is superseded. The claim R2 can make is per headroom-bound limit, unconditional on the selection (and see B-1).
**Fix:** cite CHARTER 3.7 / E-221, and state the claim's scope.

### m-4 (minor): a wrong citation

R2.md line 129: "by the rule's intent (E-296 (2))". That intent is W42-R1 section 6 step 3, not E-296 (2). **Fix:**
correct the citation.

### m-5 (minor): the records still point at W42-R1's application

`TASK_QUEUE.md` Q-189 still reads "W42-R1 applied to H4's readings", and `DRAFT_F-13` (`wt/wt-fs1`, line 589) still
reads "awaiting W42-R1's application". W42-R1's script stays runnable and refuses only if a `w42_*` output exists. If it
ran first, W42-R2 would be refused forever. **Fix:** the lead's records name `scripts/wave42_r2_apply.py`.

### m-6 (minor): efficiency of the scoring path

`extended/wave42_r2.py:130-145` rebuilds the whole rebound namespace on every `limit_estimate`, `limit_check` and
`limit_per_seed_sd` call, which is the survey's scoring path. This is correct but costly per design and per limit.
**Fix:** build it once per process and keep the identity test's freshness check.

## What I tried that found nothing

- **One code path.** `rebind` re-makes every W42-R1 function from its own code object over a copied namespace. No W42-R1
  function is decorated or closed over a table. The overrides change z to L = 7 (`present` holds seven) and make
  `_validate` require `net_month_12`.
- **The re-selected limits.** W42-R2's `_sessions_left` equals `_validate`'s `sl`. Its n_search is the per-horizon
  `sl >= h` count. n_test is `test_starts[h]`, so the per-horizon E_h is right. j_h4 is read from the readings, not a
  constant. sd_best is recomputed by W42-R1's own function, and the test holds it equal to W42-R1's.
  `limit_value` reproduces W42-R1's selection and vacuity for the six limits. `baseline_checks`, `inactivity_guards` and
  `pinned_inputs` read the corrected values.
- **The headroom formula.** For means, se_search = sd/sqrt(J_H4) is exact. For quantiles it holds asymptotically. The
  ratio form of se_test is right because the coin key includes the start, so units are independent within a seed.
  se_test and se_search are independent: disjoint seed roles, different days.
- **The do-nothing journey.** The floor refuses it exactly when its value is above zero (`bites` agrees with
  `limit_check`). The quitter's net of minus one fee unit is right. Vacuity against (-inf, inf) decides nothing and is
  reported. A negative floor is labelled "not a guard", with W42-R1's guards standing.
- **Choosable after the readings.** Nothing in the rule can be chosen after H4-C's readings are seen: no argument, no
  constant from data, one output with a refusal on any `w42_*`, and freezes on both pins.
- **The units files.** The header holds every W42-R1 required key, `net_month_12`, the id columns and
  `strict_overlaps`. `net_month_12` is extracted in the same `sl >= 252` block as `peak_oop_12_months`, with the same
  convention. The application's parse (`float`, blank as NaN) matches H4-C's, so the parity check can hold on a true
  file. It refuses a tampered measure, a foreign file, a held-out row first, and a missing sidecar record. H4-B's merge
  path rewrites through pandas, but H4-C binds the merged file's sha256, which W42-R2 checks.
- **The tests and seeds.** `tests/test_wave42_r2.py` passes in the worktree (38 tests, nothing written). No number is
  typed in the limit's definition. The test seeds are derived from names and used only on synthetic data.
