## Blocking (re-read in full)

### B-1 (Q-152): fixed

**The chain, re-read whole** (`components.py:265-314`, `triggers.py:695-713`).

Groups are taken in live order. A group is skipped when its live minute is before `free_at`. Otherwise it is placed:
- **A filled group** keeps the firm `order` from its live to the first fill bar's end, `position` to the latest exit of
  the members filled on that bar, then `gap` for five minutes.
- **An unfilled group** keeps the firm `order` to the largest `Execution.busy_until` over its members. That is the
  largest of each non-void stretch's cutoff (or the rules' walk end) and of each void re-write's live (k > 0). A member
  void at its first live never worked and adds nothing.
- The `busy` stretches tile [first live, `free_at`) without a hole, so the `next(...)` at `components.py:284` cannot
  raise.

**Why no decision can read the future.** A decision at live t compares t with `free_at`. Every term of `free_at` is fixed
by bars before t, or is at least t whatever bars come later:
- **A cutoff** is fixed by the clock.
- **A void at a re-write** reads the close of the bar stamped one minute before that re-write.
- **A fill bar or an exit at or before t** is fixed by bars before t.
- **A fill or an exit after t** stays after t when the bars from t are changed.

The latency bound (L <= 5) and the five-minute gap mean that a placement at live also implies the earlier exit was known
by the offer's alert.

**The decision-level shift test (M-5)** is `tests/test_components.py:366-455`. It replaces bars of the day stamped t or
later (index t + 1 on) with a seeded walk, or deletes them, and requires every decision with moment <= t unchanged. Its
moment is the live minute, which is the right moment for a chain decision (a manual order is aborted before it goes
live).

**The builder's tie rule: busy to the later of the two exits** (`components.py:295`). It is right, and it is needed.

- **Under the code as committed:** zero of 210 decision comparisons move on the three tie days.

**The day's end** (the builder's finding; `walker.py:250-261, 280-289`).
- `walk_end(o, rules)` is the smaller of the rules' end and the day's last present bar at or before 15:10. An order
  cannot fill on the walk's last bar.
- So a fill on the day's last present bar depends on whether a later bar exists.
- **Busy times do not depend on it:** `busy_until` uses the cutoff or `rules.walk_end`, never the data. Only fills and
  the walk do.
- **On the development store it never binds on a day that is not voided.** All 62 days whose last bar is stamped before
  15:09 CT, or in the evening, are in the manifest's `early_end_days`. Their last bars are stamped 08:14, 08:29, 11:59,

- **What the caller must void:** every `early_end_days` day, that is the calendar's holiday, `early_close` and
  `data_defect_candidate` rows.
- **More precisely,** any day whose last present bar at or before 15:10 CT has index below the walk's end: under
  `JOURNEY`, no bar stamped 15:07 or later. The calendar's own threshold (`calendar_early_closes.py:65`,
  `STORE_EARLY_END_CT = "15:00"`) is looser than that. Nothing in `schedule_day` checks it (m-3).
- The shift test's delete mode keeps the bars from `FLAT_ENTRY` on (`test_components.py:379`), so by design it cannot

### B-2 (Wave 3.1): fixed

(31) re-read whole (`journey_measures.py:329-441`) against MEASURES (31) (`docs/MEASURES.md:833-874`):

    the default, and `funded_lifetime` (`:425`) uses it for the payout curve.
