## Verdict: **must be fixed first** -- code and tests only; the pinned text stands, no re-declaration

There are three MATERIAL defects. Each is a place where the build does something other than what the pinned text
declares, in a way that moves declared rows:

- **M1.** A-11 books a trade in a TradeDay tier-1 window **at every R**, including an R at which the position had
  already closed before the window. At the strict end, a target already filled is booked as a full stop loss.
- **M2.** A-5's held reading hands the table **5 instead of 4** when Fast Pass's 45 % rule blocks and the profit since
  the request (or the balance) is not positive. This contradicts 4.9's Fast Pass row.
- **M3.** A-7's "won at R" test assumes **unshifted outcomes**:
  - in the gate's bound arm (A-8 passes one funded class to both arms), every won Quick Pay trade reads as never having
    reached the smallest R, so 5.4's bracket collapses unlabelled in the four QP gates;
  - in the replay, the verdict arm is right only if the not-yet-written runner remembers to pass the shift. Without it,
    nothing refuses.

None needs a text change. Each is a small code change with a test. Then a re-read of the diff only.

Of the ten choices the builder judged silent:
- eight are the plain reading of the text, or at least a defensible one (table below);
- A-4's mechanism carries two side effects on fixed design terms (MINOR m1);
- A-7's tolerance is sound on the arm it was written for and wrong on the others (M3).

## Findings, by severity

### MATERIAL

**M1. A-11 books a trade at every R it offers, including an R at which it had closed before the window.**
`extended/firm_counts.py` 166-191:
- `ex` holds the exits at every finite R (167);
- the trade is "open" if any R's exit is at or after t0 (176);
- then `for k in r_by: if np.isfinite(...): r_by[k][j] = book(...)` (184-191) books **every** finite R, and `pnl` with
  them.

*The text.*
- 5.5 defines when a trade is open at 12:58 (07:28): "its entry bar is stamped before 12:58 (07:28) and its exit bar at
  or after it". The exit is the one at the R it is traded at (`DayBlock.exit_by_rr`, which 5.5 itself cites).
- The bracket closes "a trade in a window ... at the window's start at the worst value it could have **while still
  open**".
- U-11 books "a trade it closes", and TradeDay liquidates open positions (`tradeday.tier1_news_auto_liquidation`).
- "At any offered R" decides the count and whether a trade is in a window. It cannot liquidate a position that, at the
  R the table chose, no longer exists.

*Evidence (Appendix A1, synthetic).* A 150-tick trade on a pinned FOMC-minutes date:
- entered on the bar stamped 12:30 CT;
- its 1R and 2R targets filled on the bars stamped 12:40 and 12:50;
- open at 12:58 at 3R only.

| reading | 1R | 2R | 3R |
|---|---|---|---|
| walked | +0.9883 | +1.9883 | -1.0184 |
| strict | **-1.0184** | **-1.0184** | -1.0184 |
| lenient | **+0.9683** | **+1.9683** | +2.9683 |

At 3R both bookings are right. At 1R and 2R the strict end turns two filled targets into full losses. The lenient end
also replaces them, by target less a tick less the flatten.

*Failure.*
- **Where.** FP's and QP's strict corners on both streams, and the strict corner the four conditional news runs start
  from.
- **What.** On a tier-1 day, an executed trade whose target at the table's R filled before the window is booked at the
  strict end as a loss of its whole risk and costs, a swing of about 1 + R in R. The red team's earlier reading puts the
  footprint on the FOMC-minutes windows.
- **Effect.** The strict corner becomes stricter than its declared end, in the direction that hurts both TradeDay
  plans' rows.
- **A-7 too.** The booked trade loses the R it had really won, so its Quick Pay bracket starts from "won nowhere".
- **The test.** T-13's own hand-built trade is exactly this case: at R <= 1 it exits at 12:50 (`tests/rd01_synthetic.py`
  689-693). But `tests/test_rd01_engine.py` 134-136 asserts only the counts and the untouched days, not "booked at the
  strict end, the lenient end and 'no liquidation'" (14.3, T-13).

*Remedy.*
- Book at an R only where `exit_by_rr[R][j] >= t0 + 1` (open at the window's start at that R). Keep the walked outcome
  and MAE at every other R.
- Book `pnl` likewise, by its own R's exit.
- T-13 asserts the per-R values of its trade at the strict end, the lenient end and "walked".

**M2. A-5's held reading also requires the profit since the request and a positive balance.**
`extended/firm_accounts.py` 404-414:
- `base` is `FundedAccount._standard_eligible`, which applies `require_profit_since_payout` (set True for FP,
  `firms.py` 84);
- line 410 adds `B > EPS`;
- `_held = base and not share_ok` (412).

*The text.*
- 4.9's rule: "**held:** when the plan's days are complete but a plan-specific gate **other than the profit since the
  last request** still blocks the request -- ... Fast Pass's 45 % rule -- the account reads `win_days` = 4 ... (the
  profit since the last request is the table's own `line_gap`)".
- 4.9's FP row: "4 while five days are counted and the 45 % rule blocks".

The profit since the request is excluded as a trigger *because* the table reads it through `line_gap`. It is not a
precondition for holding.

*Evidence (Appendix A2, synthetic).* A Fast Pass account after a first payout:
- $1,000 paid, five winning days counted;
- a best day of $900 against 45 % of its gross, so the rule blocks.

| profit since the request | the build hands the table | the text says |
|---|---|---|
| +$150 | 4 | 4 |
| -$50 | **5** | 4 |

The same happens with the balance at or below start.

*Failure.*
- **What.** In those funded states the table reads the eligible cell with a negative `line_gap` instead of the cell one
  day short, so it sizes the next session differently.
- **Where.** FP's rows move in those sessions: both corners, both streams, and FP's one-at-a-time runs.
- **The count.** 9.3's "closes blocked by the 45 % rule" (`share_blocked`, 413, counted only when held) undercounts by
  the same states.
- **How often.** The state is narrow: after a payout, with losses since it. It is real: after a payout the gross falls
  with losses, so a best day that passed the rule can start failing it.

*Remedy.*
- Held = the engine's own day count complete and `not share_ok`, whatever the profit since and the balance.
- Eligibility unchanged.
- `share_blocked` counted on the same condition.
- T-14 adds this state.

**M3. A-7 classifies "won at R" on outcomes that may be shifted; the gate's bound arm always is.**

*The code.*
- `won_at` (`firm_accounts.py` 481-493) splits a target fill from any other exit at 1.5 ticks above the fee. That holds
  only for point outcomes.
- `_through_trade` reads `point = by_rr[R][j] - R_SHIFT[R]` (553). `R_SHIFT` is set only by `with_shift` (728-734).
- `start_classes` applies it only `if s.plan == "QP" and r_shift` (`firms.py` 328).
- `run_plan` does not refuse a QP verdict-arm run without it (362-381).
- `gate_args` hands the gate the module class with `R_SHIFT = {}` (`firms.py` 186-192).
- A-8 uses **one** funded class for both the point and the `lower_bound` arm (`extended/gate.py` 329, 398-421).

*Evidence (Appendix A3, synthetic).* A trade won at every quarter R to 3R:

| case | won at 2R | R_lo | R_hi |
|---|---|---|---|
| unshifted | yes | 1.75 | 2.0 |
| shifted -0.005 R (150-tick stop, under a tick) | no | 0 | 0.25 |
| shifted -0.02 R (50-tick stop) | no | 0 | 0.25 |

*Failure.*
- **Where.** In the gate beside (10.1 item 8), the bound arm of QP-16-s, QP-16-l, QP-14-s and QP-14-l.
- **Won trades.** Every won trade is bracketed as if it peaked under 0.25R:
  - at the pessimistic end, its whole MAE after the peak is never applied;
  - a non-won trade loses its R_lo, which moves both ends.
- **Net effect.** Fewer floor rises and fewer breaches at a raised floor than 5.4 declares, with no label.
- **The replay.** The verdict arm is right only if the runner passes `r_shift` (the identity's sample does,
  `firms_identity.py` 262, 272). Given `None`, it silently misreads the same way. The only refusal is for a positive
  shift (`won_at` 491-492).

*Remedy.*
- Classify on the point outcomes on every arm. For example, the class holds the point blocks' `r_by_rr` by historical
  day: the gate's shifted list keeps the point list's order, and a replay's per-start class holds its slice. This
  replaces `R_SHIFT`.
- Or give A-8 a funded class per arm.
- Either way, refuse a Quick Pay class on a shifted arm without its point reference.
- Add a test of the bracket on a shifted arm, replay and gate.

### MINOR

**m1. A-4's seam changes two fixed design terms beyond "one micro".**
On a day-logging session (`firm_accounts.py` 336-340), A-4:
- removes the design's combine stand-aside (`sa = ... if e[0] != "combine"`);
- raises `draw.target_risk` to the log's largest risk per micro.

*The stand-aside.*
- Both designs' Combine stand-aside is the cost floor only: `min_risk_share` 0.0, `floor_ticks` 45
  (`config/fv3_corrected_portfolio.yaml` 138-140; P-14's engine call, `config/atlas_a3.yaml` 75).
- So it never stands one micro aside on the share, and dropping it is not needed for one micro.
- Dropping it removes the 45-tick cost floor. That is inert only because every stop of both designs is above it: 150
  ticks for P-16; 50 and 100 ticks for P-14 (`atlas_a3.yaml` 89).
- 2.3 holds "its sizing rule's mode, headroom, stand-aside and room cap" fixed. 4.9 asks only "one micro ... and the
  table's R". The headroom of 1.0 is needed: at a one-micro limit any smaller headroom floors to zero.

*The raised target.* It is recorded as the trade's `intended_risk`, in the ledger and in the draw's
`stats.intended_risk`. 9.3's realised-to-intended risk (E-022 (1)) is distorted on day-logging trades in its raw form;
in its allowance-capped form it reads 1.

*Remedy.*
- Keep the design's stand-aside on the replaced rule, or assert it inert.
- On logging trades, record the table's risk as intended, or leave them out of the realised-to-intended reading and say
  so.

**m2. Seeds outside section 12.**
- `tests/rd01_synthetic.py` 638 draws `SEED + 1` = **<block+3: reserved, never drawn>**, which section 12 records as "reserved, unused --
  ... never drawn, not reassigned".
- 645 draws 20262092 to 20262103, undeclared.
- `tests/test_rd01_uncertainty.py` 31, 65 and 82 draw 1, 3 and 0 for T-9's other parts. Section 12 gives T-9 <block+1: T-9's seed>.
- `tests/test_rd_firms_draft.py`'s literal search cannot see a seed written as `SEED + 1`.

No reading changes (toy data). But a pinned record ("never drawn") is now false.

*Remedy.* Derive every toy draw from <block+2: the toy seed> (`np.random.SeedSequence(<block+2: the toy seed>).spawn(n)`, or
`lockstep.child_seed(<block+2: the toy seed>, kind, i)`), and T-9's from <block+1: T-9's seed>.

**m3. 10.2 item 6 is tested on the window means, not the series.**
`extended/firm_uncertainty.py` 71 uses `np.all(means == 0.0)`. The text: "A series zero on every kept window prints
'identical on these trades'".

*Failure.* A paired 0 / +-1 series (every "ahead" measure) whose kept windows each hold equal helps and hurts prints
"identical on these trades", with no interval. That is a false description. It is most plausible at n_w 1 to 3 (month
12, the late block).

*Remedy.* Test the kept windows' values.

**m4. The identity's items 4 and 5 are weaker than 9.1's words.**

*Remedy.*
- Item 5 by the exact classes `classes_for` names, through the per-start subclasses' bases.
- The runner repeats items 4 and 5 on every replay of every run and writes them, or the identity's row says the sample

**m5. Lucid's gate is composed by hand, with no identity; no plan's gate forces the placement off.**
