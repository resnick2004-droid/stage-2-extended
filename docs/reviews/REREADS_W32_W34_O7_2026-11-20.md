## 1. Wave 3.2: the three-way runner (wt/wt-three adedf7f)

### RB-1 (CHANGES-A-CONCLUSION; blocking). An account whose room to the floor is below the next trade's costs is stranded: never breached, never replaced, still billed

**Where.**
- `extended/three_way_journey.py:241` (`_barrier`): `return np.where(Lt * t.tvn > EPS, Lt, 0.0)`, where
  `Lt = min(l, (room - e - x) / tvn)`.
- `:336` (`_trade_mc`) and `:382` (`_trade_dp`): `taken = act & (Lt > 0)`.
- The declared row `prereg/DRAFT_H3_three_way.md:125`: "no trade where the room left is at most $1e-6".
- The claims at `:126` and `extended/three_way_redraw.py:28, 183`: "so no account is stranded below a tick / a step".

**Evidence.** B-1's fix removes the whole-tick rounding, so a cushion-bound race loss now ends exactly on the floor.
The fix is right at zero cost (`tests/three_way_vendored_draw.py` now runs the journey's own step against `RandomDraw`).
The state the first read found still arises by two routes once costs are fractional or unequal:
1. **The walk when x_flat > x_stop.** The floor-side barrier uses x = max(x_stop, x_flat). A cushion-bound stop exit
   pays x_stop only, so it lands (x_flat - x_stop) dollars above the floor. H3-A's declared A1 design and every
   baseline have x_stop = 7.12 and x_flat = 12.12 (`extended/three_way_h3a.py:53`, Appendix C), so the residual is
   exactly **$5.00**. The next trade has room - e - x = 5 - 2.12 - 7.12 < 0. It is not taken, and no later trade can
   ever be taken.
2. **The race, after a full-l loss.** A loss that is not cushion-bound leaves room c' = c - e - l·tv - x_stop >= 0.
   When 0 < c' <= e + x_stop (+ EPS), every later trade has Lt <= 0.

`_settle` breaches only at `bal <= floor + EPS`, so such an account is never breached and never replaced. On an
evaluation it keeps paying the fee every billing period. That is B-1's failure, at a smaller residual. The engine does
otherwise: it takes the trade and clips it at the cushion (`vendor/stage2/propedge/campaign/lockstep.py:452-492`), so
the account ends on the floor and fails.

**Probe** (`probe_strand.py`, `probe_strand_a1.py`; `J.simulate` on the commit's code). Toy rules: mll $2000, dll
$1000, $5 a tick, three trades a session, 400 seeds, 63 sessions. Measured: the share of units whose account sits at
0 < room <= e + x at the last close, a state with no exit.

| schedule | e / x_stop / x_flat | share stranded | mean accounts bought |
|---|---|---|---|
| race, l = g = 60 | 2.12 / 2.12 / - | 0.000 | 3.98 |
| race, l = 40, g = 80 | 2.12 / 2.12 / - | 0.035 | 3.15 |
| race, l = g = 40 | **2.12 / 7.12 / -** (H3-A's) | **0.150** | 1.81 |
| race, l = 50, g = 100 | **2.12 / 7.12 / -** | **0.108** | 4.55 |
| walk s = 4, n = 40, l = g = 40 | **2.12 / 7.12 / 12.12** (H3-A's) | **0.838** | 0.003 |

On walk trades at H3-A's own costs, 84 % of units end stranded and almost no account is ever replaced. The first
read's toy (l = g = 60, equal costs) shows 0 % only because its arithmetic happens to avoid the window.

**Why the tests miss it.** `test_b1_the_forced_loss_toy_fails_and_replaces_every_account` runs at zero cost, and the
m-1 grid checks one trade's booked change, never the next trade. The exact program shares `_barrier` and `taken`, so
the simulation-against-exact comparisons cannot see it.

**The failure it allows.** The same as the first read's B-1, on the deciding reading:
- P_B, the replacement count, the fees and every later payout are misstated for every unit whose last loss lands
  inside the window;
- designs with walk trades, with larger stop-slippage gaps, or with brackets whose arithmetic hits the window more
  often are affected more than the baselines. So θ(H) differs from the declared estimand by design.

**Remedy (re-declare section 7's barrier row and R-10's walk row).**
- A floor-bound lane (its room the floor's, not the daily limit's) with room > EPS but Lt <= 0 takes the trade at
  the engine's clip: it books -room, ends on the floor and is breached and replaced at that close. The cost alone
  takes it there, which is what the engine does. A daily-limit-bound lane keeps today's rule (no trade; the day ends).
- For the walk, either:
  - put the floor-side barrier at x_stop, so a cushion-bound stop ends exactly on the floor, and clip a flatten exit
    below the floor at the floor (breach), as the engine clips; or
  - keep max(x_stop, x_flat) and apply the first rule on the next trade.
  The lead chooses one. Each changes only cost-sized amounts at the floor, and gross stays zero.
- Correct the "no account is stranded" sentences (the draft's 126, `three_way_redraw.py:28, 183`).
- **Tests:** H3-A's costs (x_flat > x_stop) and a fractional race toy, every outcome forced to lose. Assert:
  - every account failure is replaced;
  - no lane is ever alive with 0 < room_floor <= e + x at a close;
  - the same holds in `_trade_dp`.
  A mutation restoring `taken = act & (Lt > 0)` without the clip must be killed.

### RB-2 (MATERIAL; blocking). The door binds a reading to a protocol, but not the protocol to the ledger or the declaration: B-1's failing input 2 is still admitted

**Where.**

**Evidence** (`probe_protocol.py`, on `tests/search_toys`). The survey is drawn and fitted on the declared
`TY.PROTOCOL`. The counted finalist is then read on a caller-built
`Protocol("composite", (("2021-03-01", "2021-03-31"),), (7, 8, 9), 0)` and handed to `plateau.check` with that protocol
and a re-read on four other seeds. Output: **"ADMITTED, accepted = True"**. The first read's input 2 was exactly

design-counting half of B-1 is fixed (input 1 is refused as `Uncounted`), and so is the round re-draw (input 3 is
refused by `begin`). But nothing records or refuses the protocol choice:
- a caller can read the finalist on several sub-blocks or seed sets outside the ledger and hand the luckiest
  protocol to the one plateau check the ledger allows;
- `Surrogate.fit` and `proposer.propose` can likewise take a different protocol in each round.
The first read's remedy said the protocol was "taken from the declaration". Nothing in the code takes it from there.

**The failure it allows.** Selection after the fact, upstream of the k-counted test:
- the plateau verdict, and so which finalists reach the held-out test, can be bought by choosing the blocks or seeds
  after a look;
- the ledger shows nothing of it.

  declared limit.
- `plateau.check` refuses a `reread` other than `ledger_protocol.with_seeds(declared reread seeds)`.
- Optionally, the protocol carries the declaration's sha256, and `begin` checks it against the pinned file.
- **Tests:** the probe's input, refused. A round fitted on a second protocol, refused. A mutation that drops the
  ledger-protocol check, killed.

### rm-3 (MINOR). `critical_z` is a normal critical value applied to t-statistics, so the plateau's size is exceeded at small re-read seed counts

**Where.**
- `extended/plateau.py:98` (`critical_z`) and `:32` ("exactly 1 - size when every difference equals the margin");
- `:271` and `:292` (`zscore`, `paired`): z = (d - margin) / se, with se estimated from the same n paired seeds.

**Evidence** (`probe_plateau.py`). Exchangeable noise, k = 10, size 0.05, critical_z = 2.448. The share of true
plateaus accepted at the margin, by re-read seed count:

| re-read seeds | 8 | 16 | 32 | 64 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| share accepted | 0.855 | 0.911 | 0.930 | 0.941 |

The declared share is 0.95 at every count. At 16 seeds the realised size is 0.089, against the declared 0.05.

The size-and-power test uses 64 seeds and a band of three binomial standard errors at 200 repetitions (±0.046), so it
passes at 0.94 and cannot see the defect.

The equicorrelation of one half is a second, smaller assumption. A smooth design-noise field gives correlations of
0.33 to 0.47 between the differences and a size of 0.944 to 0.948, so that part is small.

**The failure it allows.** A declaration that picks fewer re-read seeds to save runs refuses true plateaus about twice
as often as it states. The memory note "check power of strict rules" applies: the cost falls on good finalists.
**Remedy.** Compute the critical value from the equicorrelated multivariate t with n - 1 degrees of freedom (one
Gauss–Hermite integral over a t, or a declared simulation). Or refuse fewer than a declared number of re-read seeds
and state the size at that number. Add a size test at the smallest seed count the declaration allows.
