## Refusal items

1. **R-10's governing clause cites a section that holds no R-10.** The clause appears at
   `prereg/H3_three_way.md:131`, `prereg/H3_three_way.md:396-397` and `tests/test_h3_draft.py:93`: "governed by
   template v3's section 7 as pinned".
   - In the design-search template text v3 (`prereg/TEMPLATE_design_search.md`, wt/wt-lit a611a65), R-10 is in
     **section 5, "The zero-edge convention"** (l.275-375).
   - Section 7 is "The pinning row: every input of 3.8 and 3.9" (l.411-434). It mentions no walk.
   - I checked every version of the file on wt/wt-lit (ab0a8e4 to 0ace458). R-10 has always been in section 5.
   - Once pinned, "where they differ, the template governs" would point at nothing, and the text's own row would govern
     by default. That is the opposite of the lead's ruling.
   - The ruling as relayed in this brief also says "section 7". The lead should confirm that section 5 (R-10) is meant.
   - **Needed:** cite template v3's R-10 by its section (5) and title, and update the phrase `test_the_form` holds.
   - **Also needed:** "as pinned" names a text that has no hash row yet. Section 16, "Refusals first" (l.353-355), does
     not require that pin before H3-0 runs. The text should fix the order: H3-0 and H3-A run only after template v3's
     hash row, and the text says what follows if the pinned R-10 differs from the walk at 805978a on S1's exits
     (`flatten`, `max_hold`). Otherwise the rule that governs H3-A can change after H3's pin.
2. **H3-D's level rule must follow the lead's ruling, not be left to H3-D's text** (`prereg/H3_three_way.md:7`, `:124`).
   - The text says the family level is declared in H3-D's text "with its rule for a k that moves before H3-D runs".
     That leaves the rule open.
   - The lead has now ruled it: the level is fixed in H3-D's text at the k current at H3-D's pin. If k moves before
     H3-D runs, H3-D's text is re-declared under a new row at the new k, never adjusted at run time.
   - **Needed:** state that rule in these two places, so the pinned H3 binds H3-D's text.
3. **The count is not written as a product and a number, and the laxity kill does not say which counts it reads**
   (`prereg/H3_three_way.md:156-162`, `:276-281`, `:253-256`).
   - The template: "k_cells = <the product of the cells' factors, written as a product and a number>; every cell is
     counted".

     pooled", with no number. R is only in Appendix A (R = 20).
   - **Power checks:** "plants P2 to P4 x the grid of sizes" (l.160) is not a defined product:
     - P2's three lever changes carry their own true delta, not `SIZE_GRID`'s.
     - P3 has `P3_EPS` (four values).

       is therefore not stated.
     - P1 is listed among the plants (l.253) but not among H3-A's checks.
   - **The kill:** l.279 says "a size count whose 95 % Clopper-Pearson interval lies wholly above its feasible level is
     a laxity". It does not say whether that reads the pooled count per part (27 per part) or every per-history count
     too (27 x 21 per part, about 1134 in all).
     - With per-history counts, an exactly sized bound would trip the kill by chance alone.
     - Either reading is conservative, but a kill read after the outcome is a choice after the fact.
   - **Needed:**
