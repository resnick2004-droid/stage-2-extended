## Findings on the runner, by severity

### MATERIAL (before the run)

**R1. The scorecard run would crash at its first paired interval.**
- `extended/firm_reading.py` 278 calls `FU.coverage(sh, int(h), int(n_w), COVERAGE_SERIES, FU.SEED)`.
- `firm_uncertainty.coverage` is `coverage(shape, *, h, n_w, n_series, seed)`, keyword-only.
- A5: `TypeError: coverage() takes 1 positional argument but 5 were given`.
- The synthetic scorecard runs with `coverage=False` (`tests/rd01_reading_synthetic.py` 123), so the path is untested.

*Failure.* The heavy `firm_scorecard` run computes TS's cells (hours), then raises in the first other cell's
`paired_rows`, the first time a reading has four kept windows. Nothing is written, but the purpose's read is ledgered,
so a rerun needs `repeat=` under a row.

*Remedy.* Call by keyword. Run the synthetic scorecard once with coverage on, at a small series count passed through.

*Cost once fixed (A6).* About 0.5 ms a series: about 6-7 minutes per (h, n_w) setting, at the five or so settings the
blocks give, cached.

**R2. 9.2's oracles are not built.**
The text says what each oracle is for:
- 9.2: "an output outside them is an engine error, never a finding";
- 11: "an oracle is breached (9.2): an engine error; the reading stops";
- 15: "the identities first ... Then the oracles, the mechanics (9.3), then the reading".

The runner has:
- a fee count (`ledger_rows`, `firm_reading.py` 385-393). It only counts (nothing refuses on it) and skips TS and LU;
- nothing else of 9.2;
- `gate_reading` returns no problems at all (`rd01_reading.py` 379).

*Missing, all refusing.*
- On every corner's zero-edge twin: the mean gross payouts per funded account against $2,000, and the pass rate per
  evaluation against 2/5, beyond their intervals (the gate purpose).
- Every request within its plan's bounds, and every net that plan's split, to 1e-9:
  - DayTraders' $50 increments and $2,000 left;
  - Fast Pass's $1,500;
  - Quick Pay's tiered split;

- Quick Pay's floor at every close: at least the end-of-day trailing floor of the same gross path, and never above
  start.

All four need data the cell discards: requests, closes and floors.

items where the runner can verify them (its reader-ledger entry, or a hashed record under the run's checkout), and the

`identity HOLDS: RD-01 9.1 item ...` lines for items 1-5 and name the commit.
