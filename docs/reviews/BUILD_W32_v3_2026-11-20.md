## 1. RB-1 and the template read's B-2 (full re-read and probes)

**Mechanism re-read in full.** I read `three_way_journey._barrier`, `_l_fill`, `_classes`, `_trade_mc`, `_trade_dp`,
`_settle`, `_close` and `_replace_accounts` at the staged index.
- **The take.** A lane takes the trade when `_l_fill * tvn > EPS`, where L_fill = min(l, (room - e - max(xs, xf)) /
  tvn), for every exit law. The take reads only the fill's state: the room, the bracket and both exit costs.
- **A lane that cannot take.**
  - Where the floor's room binds (`floor_room <= room + EPS`) and room > EPS, the lane is booked `-room`. The balance
    goes exactly to the floor, `_settle` breaches it, it is replaced at the close by `_replace_accounts`, and it is
    counted in `ffail`.
  - Where the daily limit binds, `stopped` is set and the day ends.
- **The exact program** shares `_classes`. A failure at the fill is a certain branch kept in `keep`, so no weight is
  lost.
- **The tie case.** When the floor's room is within EPS of the daily limit's room, `-room` is the smaller of the two,
  so neither the floor nor the limit is passed by more than EPS.

**Probe 1: the invariant on random toys** (`test_rt_invariant.py`).
- **Setup:** 40 random toys. Each draws tick values from 0.5 to 20, 1 to 3 contracts, unequal fractional costs with
  x_flat >= x_stop, no daily limit or a random one, payouts, a random share of walk trades at s = 1, 2 or 4 with up to
  200 steps, 20 % of trades carrying a profit level, worse fills of 0 to 2 ticks, and 0 or 10 % missed signals. Each
  runs 200 seeds over 63 sessions.
- **Instrumentation:** every call to `_classes` checks that each offered lane takes the trade, fails at the fill or
  ends its day.
- **Result:** 1,194,031 offers; **0 lanes that do none of the three**; 16,045 failures at the fill; 40,364 days
  ended. `_settle`'s floor and daily-limit refusal never fired, so no booked change passed either barrier.

**Probe 2: the re-read's own toy at H3-A's costs** (`test_rt_forced_ffail.py`, 400 seeds).

| law | mean accounts bought | mean failures at the fill per lane |
|---|---|---|
| race | 2.085 | 0.165 |
| walk | 1.255 | 0.068 |

On cycle 1's code the re-read measured 0.150 (race) and 0.838 (walk) stranded, with 0.003 accounts bought on the walk.
The build's `test_rb1_no_unit_is_stranded_at_h3as_costs` asserts that no lane stays in the window past a trade, and it
passes.

**The walk's barrier and clip.**
- The floor-side barrier is now L_race = min(l, c - x_stop), so a cushion-bound stop ends exactly on the floor.
- A time exit lies above -L_race. Its flatten cost is clipped at `-room` in both the simulation and the exact
  program, and that clip takes at most x_flat - x_stop.
- After an unclipped time exit at the lowest lattice point, the room left is rho·tv - (x_flat - x_stop). That is below
  e + max x at H3-A's step, so the next offered trade fails it at the fill. No residual band is left.
- `walk_net`'s clip at `-c_before` matches the journey's clip at `-room`. Probe 3 below covers its exactness.

**Probe 3: the walk with two fractional ends** (`probes/walk_probe.py`).
- **Setup:** 60 random draws of (G, L, s, n), with G and L fractional.
- **Exact law:** total probability 1, |gross| exactly 0 every time, and every outcome within [-L, G].
- **Agreement:** `walk_outcome` (scalar), `walk_outcomes` (vector) and `walk_run` (read in chunks of 7) on the same
  uniforms: 0 mismatches.
- **Monte Carlo:** 20,000 lanes per draw against the exact mean, largest |z| = 2.09.
- **`_p_up` by hand:** s/(s + rho_u) at the top, rho_d/(s + rho_d) at the bottom, rho_d/(rho_u + rho_d) where one
  point is both. Each is a martingale move.

**The vendored comparison.** `tests/three_way_vendored_draw.py`'s new block passes (`clip_equal`, 1200 lanes). It shows
only the engine's losing branch. The winning branch is the one row 128 declares not kept.

## 4. The findings

### m-1 (MINOR). The harness's shared monkeypatch makes the walk half of RB1's check vacuous

- **Where.** `tests/test_three_way_mutations.py`:
  - `_run` patches the mutant with the test's `monkeypatch` and passes the same object to checks of kind `"mp"`;
  - MUTANTS["RB1"] routes to `test_rb1_no_unit_is_stranded_at_h3as_costs`.
  - That test (`tests/test_three_way_runner.py` l.681) calls `monkeypatch.undo()` after its race half, which also
    removes the mutant.
- **Evidence** (`test_rt_undo.py`). The mutant `fail = ... & (t.law == 'race')` keeps the old rule for walks only.

  | the check | through the build's `_run` | the check given its own monkeypatch |
  |---|---|---|
  | `test_rb1_no_unit_is_stranded_at_h3as_costs` | **survives** | **killed** |
  | `test_rb1_forced_losses_breach_and_replace_every_account` | survives | survives (see m-2) |

- **The failure it allows.** A later edit that brings back the stranding for walk trades only passes the mutation
  suite. The behaviour holds today: the real RB1 mutant is killed by the race half.
- **Remedy.** Patch mutants through a `pytest.MonkeyPatch()` of the harness's own and undo it after the check. Or make
  the check re-apply `_forced` without `undo()`. Add a walk-only RB1 mutant routed to the walk half.

### m-2 (MINOR). The forced-loss RB-1 test never reaches a failure at the fill

- **Where.** `tests/test_three_way_runner.py`, `test_rb1_forced_losses_breach_and_replace_every_account`.
- **Evidence** (`test_rt_forced_ffail.py`). At its own inputs (mll 2000, dll 1000, l = 40, one loss of $209.24), both
  laws give `accounts = [15, 15, 15, 15]` and `fill_failures = [0, 0, 0, 0]`. Each day's fourth loss is cushion-bound on
  the daily limit, and the next day's on the floor. So it is the cycle-1 B-1 test at H3-A's costs, not a test of the
  ruling.
- **Failure it allows.** None today: the random-seed test and `test_b2_...` hold the rule. But the docstring claims a
  property the test does not exercise.
- **Remedy.** Choose a floor where full-l losses land in (EPS, e + max x], such as mll = 9 × 209.24 + 10 with no daily
  limit. Assert `fill_failures > 0` and that every such failure is replaced. Route a mutant to it.

### m-3 (MINOR, latent). The profit level's unit contradicts itself

- **Where.**
  - `extended/three_way_journey.py` l.89 ("a rule's profit level in net ticks") and `g_eff`.
  - `three_way_redraw.walk_barriers`' docstring ("in net ticks").
  - Draft row 130 ("min(g, level), in net ticks").
  - The booking `G * t.tvn - xt` (l.400, l.448), which `test_m2_a_profit_level_races_at_min_g_level` pins (it asserts
    `-1.0 + G*5 - 0.5`).
- **Failure it allows.** A firm's runner passes the engine's need, which is net dollars (`need_to_pass`), as level =
  need / tvn, as the docstring invites. Each need-aware exit then nets x_target short of the need. A trade meant to
  pass the account leaves it x_target below the target, so the pass is lost that day.
- **Remedy.** Either make `level` gross ticks and say so (the caller converts (need + x_target) / tvn), or convert in
  `g_eff`. Then close the template read's m-3 in the same words. Add a test with a level at a pass that must pass.

### m-4 (MINOR). Two code paths that no test reaches

My mutants were run through the build's `_mutant` and `_killed`, against ten relevant tests, each with its own
monkeypatch (`test_rt_extra_mutants.py`).
- **(a) The journey's walk with a level.** Sending `float(t.g)` to `walk_run` (simulation) or to `walk_law` (exact
  program) in place of `G` **survives**. Only the oracle `walk_law` is tested at a fractional G
  (`test_m2_a_fractional_target_in_the_walk_is_raced_exactly`).
- **(b) The walk's clip where the daily limit binds.** Clipping at `-(bal - floor)` instead of `-room` **survives** in
  both paths. On a limit-bound flatten it would make `_settle` raise its "REFUSED (B-1, M-4)" refusal, so the error
  would be loud at run time, never silent.
- **Killed:**
  - the exact program's walk clip removed (by `test_rb1_the_walks_flatten_...`);
  - `floor_bound` dropped, the take at L_race, the failure booking off by $0.001, and the day-end dropped (by `test_b2_...`);
  - the race's win at g (by `test_m2_a_profit_level_...`).
- **Remedy.** Add a walk trade carrying a level to the journey's walk tests, in both the simulation and the exact
  program. Add a test where a flatten's clip falls on the daily limit.

### n-1 (NOTE). Certain failure where a driftless race exists

- **Where.** `_classes`: the take uses L_fill, which nets both exit costs, while the walk now has its own clip.
- **What happens.** For a room in (e + x_stop, e + max x], L_race > 0, yet the lane is failed with certainty. The race
  (or the engine) would win with probability L_race / (L_race + G).
- **Measured** (`test_rt_band.py`, the RB-1 toy, 400 seeds).

  | law | failures at the fill in the band | expected wins forgone | gross per event |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | race | 4 of 66 | 0.041 | at most x_flat - x_stop = $5 |
  | walk | 9 of 27 | 0.098 | at most x_flat - x_stop = $5 |

- **Status.** Row 128 declares it ("the engine's winning branch ... is not kept"), and E-247 rules the take at L_fill.
  So no change is needed.
- **For the lead.** Once the walk clips its own flatten, a take at L_race > EPS would also read no exit, would be
  driftless, and would match the engine. That choice is open in the template's v2.

### n-2 (NOTE). The daily limit binding below the costs

- **What happens.** The day ends with nothing booked. The engine would take the trade, clip at the limit and book
  -room, at most e + max x.
- **The edge case.** If dll <= e + max x, the lane never trades and never fails. TOY_RULES' dll is $800 against at most
  $34.24 of costs at +2 ticks, so this cannot happen at the declared inputs.
- **Remedy.** A firm's run should check dll > e + max x (+ the worse fill) at construction.

### n-3 (NOTE). The count is kept but not reported

- `Records.fill_failures` is kept per lane. No function in `three_way_runner` or `three_way_h3a` reports it, although
  row 128 says "reported beside".
- `_merge` drops `ffail` (it is not in `_state_key`), so `expect` could not report it either.
- **Remedy.** Wire it into H3's report step when that is built.

### n-4 (NOTE). The excluded sliver of room

- `fail = ... & (room > EPS)` leaves a floor-bound lane with room in (0, EPS] neither taking, failing nor ending its
  day.
- `_settle` breaches at bal <= floor + EPS, so the only way in is a payout at the close on a balance under about 2e-6.
  Probe 1's count was 0.
- **Remedy.** Drop the condition: booking `-room` then breaches. Or assert the state unreachable.
