### MINOR (before the run: printed outputs, read once)

**D3. The zero-edge twin's martingale tally counts every account twice.** `pp_run.py:1057-1073`, 1105-1115.

- **Evidence.**
  - `GateTally` accumulates gross payouts "every shift".
  - For the twin, `gate_call` zeroes `e_by` and `lb_by`. `atlas_a3.gate_kwargs` then sets `e = lb = 0`, so the bound
    arm is the point arm shifted by zero, at the same seeds: the same paths.
- **What it allows.** n is doubled with duplicated samples, so the standard error comes out about 1/√2 of its true
  value. The 3 standard-error tolerance of 8.2 then becomes about 2.1, and a false refusal after the purposes are
  spent becomes more likely. (The previous version tallied the point shift only.)
- **Remedy.** Tally the twin's gross on the point shift only, and test it with a twin whose two arms are identical.

**D4. The holiday-hours reach misses the eve of every holiday with no session.** `pp_run.py:184-192`.

- **Evidence.**
  - `eve = {dates[di[d] - 1] for d in holidays if d in di ...}` finds an eve only for a holiday that has a session.
  - E-029's calendar has 15 holidays with "no day": Good Friday, Christmas and New Year's Day (C.3).
  - For 12 of them, the last session before is a normal session not otherwise in the set. Examples: 2020-04-09,
    2020-12-31, 2022-12-23, 2024-03-28 and 2025-12-31.
- **What it allows.** Requests at "the last session before a scheduled holiday" (8.3, 13350348) on those 12 sessions
  are never counted.
- **Remedy.** Each holiday's eve is the last date in `dates` before it, whether or not the holiday has a session. Add a
  test with a Good Friday.

**D5. An E-015 limit is admitted from any row that mentions E-015, with its bound typed on the command line**
(reading e). `pp_reading.py:96-115`.

- **Evidence.**
  - `parse_limits` asks only that the named row exist in the checkout and contain "E-015". The column, kind and bound

  - Being in the checkout does prove "before the commit". Mentioning E-015 does not prove an approval, nor its value.
- **What it allows.** A flag on the menu computed from a bound that no approval states.
- **Remedy.** Read the limit from the approving row itself, with a token such as `E-015-limit:` followed by the
  column, `max` or `min` and the bound, written only in the row recording the operator's approval. No typed bound.
  Today none is approved, so the menu prints "none approved".
