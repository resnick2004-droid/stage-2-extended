### R3-2 (MINOR). `h4_sidecar_sha256` hashes the checkout's bytes, which Git's line-ending conversion changes on this machine

**Where:** `wave42_apply.py` 135-136 (`side_sha = sha256_bytes(side_bytes)`); declaration 102-103 ("its

H4's collection row records it" fails on a correct application, or passes only if both sides happened to hash the same
form. The failure protocol forbids a rerun until it passes, so the mismatch would cost a diagnosis cycle at the pin.

**Remedy:** say what is hashed. Either:
- (wording) 1.1: "`h4_sidecar_sha256` is the sha256 of the sidecar's **git blob** (LF), and the pinning row cites the
  blob's hash", with the script's code change below; or

The cleaner fix is a code change and so a cycle: hash `side_bytes.replace(b"\r\n", b"\n")`, or add
`results/*.manifest.json -text` to `.gitattributes`. That attributes change is infrastructure outside this rule. The
lead chooses which.

### n3-4 (NOTE). Removing the monotonicity refusal is equivalent, and in fact stricter

The cycle-1 refusal said counts must be positive and non-increasing in the horizon. It is replaced by two conditions:
- counts must equal `complete_starts(sessions, h)` with `sessions` an integer (`wave42.py` 288-292);
- counts must be at least 1 (284), which forces `sessions` to be at least 252.

strictly decreasing, and the old refusal can never fire on an input the new checks accept. The accepted sets are a
subset of the old ones.

`q_factor`'s stratum counts (`test_starts[h] - test_starts[next]`) are therefore never negative. The builder's mutant
that removes the `complete_starts` equality is killed, so the guard that now carries monotonicity is tested.

## 5. Re-derivations

### TEST_SEEDS = 1000 at k = 13 (independent)

**The setup.** alpha = 0.05 / 182 = 2.747e-4 and c_h = sqrt(2 ln(1 / alpha)) = 4.050. Take four pairs at
delta = `DELTA_MIN` = 0.01 and sigma = 1. The union bound needs each miss at most 0.05, so z = 1.645, and
J >= Q1 (c_h + z)^2 / delta^2. Q1 is the per-seed sum of a_u^2 over the nested strata, at equal weights 1/6 and
horizons money 63, speed, cost and ruin 126, bankroll and steady 252.

**The pinned held-out calendar** has **657 sessions**: its row count, a dates-only read. Its complete starts are 595
(63), 532 (126) and 406 (252).

| sessions | k = 13 | 15 | 17 | 20 | fallback (P_B alone), k = 13 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 663 (weekdays) | 604 | 619 | 632 | 648 | 540 |
| **657 (the pinned file)** | **611** | 626 | 639 | 655 | 545 |
| 617 (40 fewer than the weekdays) | 662 | 678 | 692 | 710 | 585 |

**The conclusions:**
- At J = 1000 and the pinned 657 sessions, the conjoint lower bound is 0.9976 at k = 13 and 0.9963 at k = 17. In the
  fallback form it is 0.9995.
- 1000 / 611 = 1.64, so "at least 1.5 times the need at k = 13" holds at the real count. It also holds at 623 sessions
  (the test's lower count): 1000 is at least 1.5 x about 654.
- J = 1000 meets the target at every k from 13 to 59.
- **sigma = 1 is a valid bound** on the per-unit sd: each unit's composite difference is a weighted sum of terms in
  [-1, 1], whose variance is at most (the sum of its weights)^2. **delta\* >= `DELTA_MIN`** and **delta_b >= delta\***,
  so `J_worst_case` bounds `J_needed`.

The claim stands. What I did not re-derive is the three-way note's premise that held-out units are independent given
the history (section 7).

### The do-nothing floor

**The composite form, dimension by dimension:**
- **bankroll:** x = 0 at or below lo (lo >= 0 in every branch of `_caps`), so z = 1;
- **ruin:** spend 0 is at or below B\*, and B\* is at least one fee unit, which is positive; so not run out, z = 1;
- **money:** net 0 is not ahead, so 0;
- **speed:** W + 1 is at or above hi (hi <= W + 1 in every branch), so 0;
- **cost:** unpassed, so z = 0 (the override at `wave42.py` 603);
- **steady:** 0.

That gives **2/6 exactly, whatever H4 reads.** The fallback (money alone) gives **0**. The quitter is at most 2/6, so
the floor at the do-nothing journey is the larger of the two. Probe P-e: 0.3333 at fees 1, 49, 149 and 1e4, with the
quitter 0.333, 0.317, 0.282 and 0.167. Correct.
