### W-1 (CHANGES-A-CONCLUSION). The partial regimes are unbounded and the regime count was dropped, so a one-switch side passes

**Where.**

  - `gaps = np.diff(pts)` checks only interior gaps, and a single point has none;
  - `B = len(pts) + 1` is whatever the family declares.

**The evidence** (deterministic; my `v6a.py`, through the drafter's own `regime_filter_check`). The floor at small B is
zero: `regime_floor(2)` = `regime_floor(7)` = 0.
- **One switch.** The side is long for 328 sessions and short for 329, with the single point [328]. It passes
  `(True, [])` in **every** band: below 42, 42 to 50, and 51 to 62.
- **Three regimes of 219.** The points are 219, 263, 307, 351, 395 and 438, so the interior gaps are 44 and 43 and the
  partials 219 each. B = 7, the floor is 0, and it passes in 42 to 50. With points 219, 274, 329, 384 and 438 it passes
  in 51 to 62.
- **T-0's four constructions** hold only because `gamed_sides` covers the span with points. T-0 never tries the
  adversary's best declaration.

**The failure it allows.**
- V-1's size hole stays open: a family whose realised side is one switch, or two or three fixed regimes, gets a
  one-sample line.
- 2.5's new text says otherwise, and so do 10.4 and B-33.
- Under the second column (n-4, V-5) this side passes for any family, with R = 2. The switching side's effective floor
  there is R >= 5.

**Remedy (rule).**
- **(a) Spacing.** Every interior regime has a length in the band, and the span's first and last partial regimes are
  each at most the band's upper end.
  - In code: refuse unless `pts` is non-empty, `pts[0] <= hi` and `n - pts[-1] <= hi`.
  - With this, B lies in `regime_blocks(n, band)`, which is 14 to 17 and 11 to 14 at 657. Interior gaps in [lo, hi]
    and partials in [1, hi] give ceil(n/hi) <= B <= floor((n - 2)/lo) + 2, which matches `regime_blocks`.
  - Restate the count condition beside it anyway: B within `regime_blocks(n, band)`.
- **What I checked** (`v6c.py`, illustration):
  - Every phase of every flip in all three bands passes the cap (0 failures). The bound and the printed refusals are
    unchanged.
  - The single-point and six-point declarations are refused ("partial").
  - The best covering declaration for the one-switch side is refused by the runs floor in 42 to 50 (B = 14) and in
    51 to 62 (B = 12).
- **T-0.** Add the two adversarial declarations, the single point [328] and the six points 219..438, as refused cases.
- **10.4.** Map the deletion of the count condition.

### W-2 (MATERIAL). In the band below 42, spacing in [1, 41] permits crowded points, and a two-halves side passes

**Where.** 2.5 (a), "has a length in the band", where the band's lower end is 1.

**The evidence** (`v6c.py`, with W-1's cap applied). The side is long for 328 sessions, then has 5 daily changes,
then is short for the rest: R = 6, mean side 0.005.
- The declared points are every 41 sessions, plus the 5 change points.
- B = 21 and the floor is 5. It passes as a below-42 regime filter.
- As a below-42 switching side it would need R >= 9 (`band_interval(657, 'below 42')`).
- The same gap of 1 lets the edge-reversal side (three regimes of 219, R = 9) pass as a below-42 regime filter. 2.5
  claims spacing refuses it, but that claim is stated only for 42 to 50, so it is not false as written.

**The failure it allows.**
- In the first column, the second statistic's row in the band below 42 admits sides with two-halves persistence and a
  short burst, at R = 6.
- These are not draws of any single-length flip.
- Under the second column it adds nothing below the switching floor of R >= 5.

**Remedy (rule).**
- The interior gaps lie within a factor of 1.5 of each other: the largest is at most 1.5 times the smallest.
- This binds only below 42: 50/42 and 62/51 are under 1.5, so the other bands are unaffected.
- It admits calendar regimes. Month-like gaps of 19 to 23 sessions have a ratio of about 1.21.
- Every flip phase passes. The crowded side above is refused (`v6e.py`).
- Class the constant 1.5 as **fixed before any read, from sides only**, as an unlabelled choice made now. Equal spacing
  at the declared L, plus or minus a declared tolerance, is an equivalent alternative.

- Under the flip model the B - 1 boundary signs are independent and fair, so the dense count of changes is exactly
  Bin(B - 1, 1/2).
