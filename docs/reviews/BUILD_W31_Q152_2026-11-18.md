## Blocking

### B-1 (Q-152). The chain frees the firm on a re-written ticket that turns void, so a later offer is placed or skipped on bars after its live minute

- **Where:**
  - `extended/triggers.py:674-680`: `Execution.ended` returns the *last* attempt's live when that attempt is void.
  - `extended/components.py:281-283`: `placed = [e.ended for e in exs if e.trade.fill.status != "void"]`. An execution
    whose last attempt is void is dropped from `placed`, even when an earlier attempt worked for a stretch.
- **The mechanism:**
  - An RL3 ticket goes live at 09:00 and works until a news window's start (attempt 1, cancelled).
  - After the window it is re-written. At the re-write it is already through, so it is void (attempt 2).
  - The group's status is then "void", so `free_at` is not moved. The firm counts as free from 09:00, although an
    entry order worked from 09:00 to the window's start.
  - Groups are taken in live order. A later component's offer with live inside 09:00 to the window's start is then
    placed. With the re-write not void, the same offer would be skipped (`order`).
  - **Whether an offer at minute t is placed therefore depends on the price after the window, which can be tens of
    minutes after t.** That is look-ahead in the chain. It also breaks the chain's own rule ("no other entry order is
    working", F-04 5.5, Stage 2 D-429). The invariant check at `components.py:284-286` tests positions only, so it
    passes.
- **Failing input (run, scratchpad `chk_rewrite_void.py`):**
  - Prior day: one bar stamped 14:59 at 13100, so `pdc` = 13100, a resistance above `P_ref` 13050.
  - Day: flat at 13050. A news `Window(1000, 1010)` (09:40-09:50 CT).
  - Design: `Component("rl3", "ny_am", 960, 960, RL3Tickets("pdc", 14:30))` and
    `Component("mk", "ny_am", 961, 995, P16Marks((970, 980, 990)))`. Unit seed 1.
  - Unchanged bars: the ticket is `cancelled:cutoff`, and the offers at 970, 980 and 990 are skipped (`order`).
  - With only the bars stamped 1005 and later moved to 13200: the ticket is `void:through` at its 09:51 re-write, and
    **the offer at 970 is traded** (980 and 990 are skipped as `position`).
  - So the decision at 09:30 changed because of bars from 09:45 on.
- **Reach:**
  - It needs a one-member group (`pdc`, or a kind whose other ticket was voided at admission), a news window between
    09:00 and the cutoff, and a second component.
  - Topstep's windows on the record today fall before 09:00 on a non-FOMC day (RL3 4.6), so it is dormant there.
  - Another firm's record (`release_window`, e.g. `mffu.news_tier1_rule`) or a widened Topstep record makes it live.
- **Remedy:**
  - The group occupies the firm until the last stretch that worked has ended: the largest over attempts of each
    non-void attempt's end (its cutoff, or the walk's end), or the void attempt's live, since the order worked up to
    the window.
  - Use that value in `placed`, including executions whose last attempt is void.
  - Add a schedule-level shift test (see M-5).

### B-2 (Wave 3.1). (31)'s Kaplan-Meier risk set counts an account censored at count k as surviving the step k to k+1

- **Where:**
  - `extended/journey_measures.py:384-390`: `n_k = w[t >= k].sum()`, so a censored account with count k is in the
    denominator of the step k to k+1 and never in its numerator. The docstring says so: "a censored one stays in the
    risk set at its own time and leaves after it".
  - `tests/test_journey_measures.py:240-248` hard-codes that convention: `S(2) = 4/5 x 3/4`.
- **Why it is wrong:**
  - The "time" is a count of payouts. A lost account with count k was lost between its k-th and (k+1)-th payout. A
    censored account with count k is known only to have reached k; it may yet be lost before its (k+1)-th.
  - MEASURES.md (31) defines `S(k)` as the chance an account reaches its k-th payout before loss, "with censored
    accounts leaving the risk set at their count".
  - The continuous-time tie rule the code uses (a censoring at t outlives an event at t) does not carry over to a
    count: it books every censored account as having survived the step it is in.
  - The bias is upward, and it is largest exactly where (31) is read: accounts funded late in the window and still
    open with 0 or 1 payouts.
- **Failing input (run, scratchpad `chk_km.py`):** twelve equal-weight accounts:
  - 4 lost with 0 payouts, 4 still open with 0, 2 lost with 1 and 2 open with 1.
  - The code gives `S(1) = 2/3` and a **median of 1 payout**.
  - With the censored accounts out of the step at their count, `S(1) = 1/2` and the **median is 0**.
  - On the module's own hand case, `S(2)` is 0.600 against 0.533.
- **Remedy:**
  - The risk set for the step k to k+1 is the weight with count > k, plus the weight lost at count k.
  - Re-derive the hand test from the definition.
  - Keep `curve_sessions` (a time in sessions) on the usual convention, or state its tie rule.
  - If the lead prefers the actuarial half-weight, it is a row, written into (31)'s text.

### M-1 (Wave 3.1). `P_B` mixes two records

- **Where:** `extended/user_readings.py:282, 296-300`.
- **What happens:** "ahead at month m" is `U._net_at(V, 21m)`, from the exact-length run wherever its events differ
  (`Views.at`). The peak tested against B is the long run's `peak_month`.
- **Failing input:** a start whose exact 63-session run differs from the long run reads its month-3 net from one

- **Why the test misses it:** `w31_synthetic.py`'s "`P_B` equals `p_bankroll` recomputed from `rel`" holds only
  because no start differs there.
- **Remedy:** read both from one record. Or refuse `P_B` when any start of `E_63` has `differs` non-empty.

### M-2 (Wave 3.1). The journey rows rely on an identity that is reported, not enforced

- **Where:** `extended/journey_readings.py:88-91`.
- **What happens:** every `journey` row reads the long run cut at the block's end. MEASURES.md 1.1 allows that only
  "with U1's identity checked on the cut runs". The build reports `exact_runs_differing` and refuses nothing.
- **Under a response other than `off`:** the check runs on every 21st start only (`EXACT_EVERY`), so 20 in 21 starts
  are unchecked.
- **Remedy:**
  - Refuse a reading at h when a checked start of `E_h` differs up to h.
  - Run the held-out runner with `exact_every=1`.

### M-3 (Wave 3.1). A default (search-years) call still computes and returns the held-out starts' journeys

- **Where:** `extended/user_beside.py:782-879` and `extended/user_readings.py:385`.

- **Failing input:** a search caller that passes every start of the job gets the held-out starts' nets, payouts and

### M-4 (Q-152). The news-window flatten is booked at the close before the window, unlike either declared text

- **Where:** `extended/triggers.py:242-246, 621-627`.
- **What the build does:** books the close of the bar stamped `first - 1`, with no price slippage. The two flatten
  ticks are charged only when `costs` is given, so `ScheduledTrade.gross_ticks` carries none.
- **What the declared texts say:**
  - RL3 4.6 and 5.4: "at that bar ... at the worst of that bar's prices less two ticks".
  - E-200 R-1 / RD-05 (`extended/modes_news.py:16-18, 94-99`): the open of the bar stamped at the window's first minute,
    with the profile's flatten slippage.
- **The unscheduled flatten** (`first + 1`) closes at the release bar's own close. RL3 4.7 (d) says at the first bar
  after the release minute.
- **Failing input:** a long position open at a 10:00 window whose bar stamped 10:00 trades 20 ticks lower. RL3's text
  books that low less two ticks; the build books the 09:59 close.
- **The direction is optimistic.** It is an unlabelled choice.
- **Remedy:** take RD-05's rule (the one CHARTER 4 cites), in price, with a row naming it.

### M-5 (Q-152). No test can catch B-1

- **Where:** `tests/test_triggers.py:219-258`; `tests/test_components.py`.
- **What is missing:**
  - The shift test covers offers, not the scheduler's decisions.
  - The chain's own check (`components.py:284-286`) tests positions, not orders.
- **Remedy:** for every placed or skipped group at live t, replace the bars stamped at t or later (as
  `sb.replace_from` does) and require the same place-or-skip decision. Run it on days with news windows after 09:00

### M-6 (Q-152). A same-bar fill of two one-cancels-other tickets books no trade and frees the firm at that bar

- **Where:** `extended/components.py:269-272`.
- **What actually happens:** at the broker one ticket fills and cancels the other inside the bar. So one position
  exists, and which one is unknown.
- **What the build does:** books none, and sets `free_at` to the tie bar's end. A later component may then trade while
  the real user still holds a position.
- **Why the direction is optimistic:** the bars dropped are the widest. Under the declared fill-bar rule, a stop touch
  on a resting fill's bar is a loss, so they would be mostly losses.
- **Failing input:** `test_rl3_one_cancels_other_and_a_same_bar_tie`'s tie bar. There, a second component live at
  10:21 would be placed.
- **Remedy:** book the worse of the two members' walks, or keep the firm busy until the later member's exit. Count the
  ties either way.

### M-7 (Q-152). Skips are counted under the wrong reason

- **Where:** `extended/components.py:259, 277-278`.
- **What happens:** a resting order that works from 09:00 and fills at 10:40 sets `why_busy = "position"`. Offers
  skipped while it was only *working* (09:00 to 10:40) are counted `position`, not `order`.
- **Effect:** the decisions are right but the counts are wrong. Those counts are supply's inputs ((37) to (39)).
- **Remedy:** classify each skip by the state at its live minute.

### M-8 (Q-152). The closed 08:30-08:59 window is applied to every source, beyond the cited row

- **Where:** `extended/triggers.py:62-63, 272`.
- **What happens:**
  - The window applies to P-14, P-16 and RL3 alike on every day. It refuses every P-14 offer live 08:35 to 08:59, and
    it will flatten any overnight position at 08:30 once H2 opens overnight sources.
  - The cited words (E-202 (2)(a); CHARTER 4 as amended) are "RL3's 08:30-08:59 fill window stays closed".
  - The triage's Q-1xx row plans the library "with ... the closed 08:30-08:59 window", but no row rules it for the
    other sources. The coin-sided P-14 source then differs from baseline P-14's timing.
- **Remedy:** a row naming the window's reach for each source, or a per-source switch reported beside.

- **Look-ahead in the sources:**
  - P-14's event slot is the minute's end, and its reference bar completes at (b+1)k.
  - A limit's level is `price_at(alert)`, the close of the bar stamped alert - 1.

  - The void at live reads the close of the bar stamped live - 1, the broker's own check.
  - Moving each cut by one minute in my head breaks the leaky harness and none of the sources. The shift tests cover
    a roll day, a post-DST day and random cuts.
- **Latency, the windows and the walk:**
  - A market order fills at the open of the first bar stamped at or after alert + L. A limit cannot fill before it.
  - The latency-free sources meet max(L) = 5.
  - A limit fills only on a trade-through (`low <= P - tick`), at P and never better. A touch does not fill.
  - A stop fills at its price plus a tick, or at the gap open plus a tick.
  - The closed window covers slots 930-959. RL3 is live at 960.
  - BLS covers bars stamped 17:00-07:35, so the earliest fill is the open of the bar stamped 07:36.
  - FOMC and calendar voids refuse every offer.
  - A window's flatten index ends the walk at the window's start. The cutoff index is `last + 1`. RL3's expiry is
    cutoff - 1.
- **The coin:**
  - The key's layout equals `three_way_arith.seed_key`. It carries the start and seed index, and no account or firm.
  - MNQ and NQ are one underlying, and every word is below 2^32.
  - The moment is unique per (session, decision, sub). P-14 cannot emit two events at one slot from one reference bar.
  - RL3's seven subs are distinct. Same-moment sharing across sources and firms is intended (m-9).
  - The uniform's grid makes `U < 0.5` exactly half. Distinct keys give distinct states.
  - The position's side equals the coin's for every order kind (`order_for`).
- **Opposite sides:** `UserBook` refuses any overlap of [open, close) on the absolute clock, opposite sides as tier 1.
  Within a firm the chain prevents overlapping positions (B-1 is about orders).
- **The block bound (`journey_window`):**
  - The last-index scan is right.
  - The long run and every exact run stop inside the start's block.
  - Dates in neither block, out of order, or from 2026-07-17 on are refused before any start.
  - A payout arriving after the block's last close is outside `rel`.
- **Every measure reads closes 0 .. h:** the `closes()` guard is used everywhere, and (31) reads `ended_on` and arrivals
  only up to W.
- **The strict rule:** I checked sufficiency. With every censored lower bound strictly above the percentile, and the

  N-1 cases hold.
