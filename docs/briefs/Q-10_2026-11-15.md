## The goal
A walker in this repository's package (`extended/walker.py`; create `extended/__init__.py` minimally if it is not
there -- another worker is building the package's `results` part on another branch, so keep `__init__.py` to what you
need and say what you put in it) that, given 1-minute NQ bars and an order, returns the trade's outcome at each R:

- **orders:** a market entry at a bar's open; a resting **stop** entry live from `t_live` at a price (fills on the first
  bar from `t_live` that trades at it, at the stop price plus `slip_ticks`, or at the bar's open plus slippage if it
  opens beyond -- gap-through); a resting **limit** entry live from `t_live` (fills only on a bar that trades **through**
  the level by at least one tick, at the limit price, never better); a ticket void if already through at `t_live`;
  cancelled at a cutoff if unfilled; at most one fill;
- **the walk:** stop and targets at each R from the fill price; on a later bar touching both, **stop first**; on the
  fill bar of a resting order a stop touch is a loss and a target touch is not counted; a trade open at 15:10 CT is
  flattened at that bar's close (unresolved); costs as Stage 2 charges them (`vendor/stage2/scripts/atlas_a3.py`
  `fee_by_stop`, `vendor/stage2/config/atlas_a3.yaml` `slip_ticks`, `vendor/stage2/config/fills.yaml`), the stop exit
  slipping a tick, a limit target none -- **check each convention against Stage 2's own walker** (`atlas_a3.walk_minutes`,
  `vendor/stage2/propedge/outcomes/atlas_core.py`, `bracket_core.py`) and say where the declaration's words and
  Stage 2's code differ;
- **the mirror:** the opposite side at the same fill bar and price with the same stop, targets and rules.
