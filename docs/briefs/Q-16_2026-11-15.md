## 1. The trades-first test (the pass line, E-008)
The statistic: a design's lift over its coin twin on the same brackets -- per trade, half the difference between the
rule side's outcome and its mirror's (in win probability, and in net R beside) -- on the judged block (2024-01-02 to
2026-07-16), tested one-sided at **0.05/(k(k+1)) for k = 11 to 15**. Trades cluster within sessions and outcomes are
dependent across nearby sessions; cells can be sparse (a few hundred trades). Build, in this repository's package
(`extended/`), a test that **delivers its level** under that dependence. Candidates to compare: Student-t on session-block
means (block 21 sessions, as F-01 declared), a studentised moving-block bootstrap with enough draws for the tail (or a
tail estimate that does not need them), and a conservative fallback. Calibrate each by simulation:

- **Nulls with the real dependence:** a sign-flip null on real coin-twin pairs -- take P-16's own registered trades
  (Stage 2's coin, already read by Stage 2; its trades come from the vendored `rc150_dev_test.build_cells()` or the
  walks in `vendor/stage2/data/atlas`) and flip the sign of every pair difference by session block, so the dependence
  stays and the mean is zero by construction; plus synthetic nulls with declared intra-session clustering and
  across-session autocorrelation. Reading P-16's trades this way reads no new design's outcome.
- Report each candidate's rejection rate at each level with its Monte Carlo interval (enough replications that the
  interval can tell 0.05/132 from twice it -- say how many and why), and its power curve: the minimum detectable lift
  at 80 % power for trade counts of 200, 500, 1,000, 2,000 and 5,000.
- Pick the one that holds its level with the most power; ship it with a test of its size on a fixed null (fast,
  small) and the full calibration as a `slow` test or a script.
