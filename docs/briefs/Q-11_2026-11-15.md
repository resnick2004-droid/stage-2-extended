## 1. The library (`extended/features.py`)
30-50 features at an alert time t (the 5-minute marks of US regular hours), computed only from bars stamped
`<= t - 1 min`, normalised (rolling ATR, the session's own range, time-of-day medians computed from the past only),
never raw price levels: e.g. returns and ranges over several windows in ATR units, position in the day's and the
prior day's range, distance to the overnight and prior-day levels in ATR units, opening-range breadth, volume against
its time-of-day median, realised volatility ratios, gap from the prior close, time of day, day of week, release-day
flags known in advance. One line of documentation each (formula, window, normaliser, the bars it reads), one
registry of all of them.

**Tests (synthetic bars, fixed seeds in the tests):** the shift-invariance test for every feature (every bar stamped
`>= t` replaced by arbitrary values changes nothing at t), over many t including the session's first minutes, a
contract roll and a DST change; no feature all-NaN or constant on a realistic synthetic session; the time-of-day
medians and every normaliser shown to use the past only (a test that perturbs the future of the whole series and
re-checks); performance acceptable on a full synthetic year (state the time).
