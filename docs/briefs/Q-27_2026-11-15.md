## The goal
`scripts/build_report.py` (code in `extended/report.py`) reads result files through `extended.results.read_result`
(manifests verified first) and writes `docs/report/` -- a Markdown page with charts (PNG) the operator, who is not a
quant, can read in a few minutes. For each design in the file:

1. **The journey table:** every E-014 variable (sessions and cash to the first pass and to the first payout, sessions to
   break-even and the share falling back, the evenness measures) as median, p10 and p90, with plain-English labels.
2. **A fan chart** of cumulative net cash by session since the start, across start days (median line, p10-p90 and
   p25-p75 bands), break-even marked.
3. **The break-even curve:** the share of starts at or above break-even by session.
4. **Payouts:** the distribution of payout sizes and of the gaps between payouts; the largest month's share.
5. **The evenness trade-off:** designs placed by share ahead against an evenness measure.
6. **The denial ladder** beside (0, 10, 25, 50 %) when the file holds it.
7. Every page says what is **not modelled** (CHARTER section 2) and labels exploratory files as exploratory.

Charts: clear, labelled axes in plain words, readable in light and dark backgrounds, colour-blind-safe; no chart without
its source file named under it.

**Every number in the page is computed from its file** and registered in `extended/trace.py`, so
`scripts/trace_check.py` passes on the built page.
