## 3. Findings, most serious first

**F-1 (blocking; E-288). H4-0 and H4-S ran on a build whose only review says BLOCK, with no re-review on record.**
The manifests' commit `31c507e` ("H4 post-pin build") carries `docs/reviews/BUILD_H4_b7c2afe_2026-11-22.md`, whose
verdict (its section 2) is "**BLOCK** -- Must fix before merge or running any H4 part". The commit message says it
"resolves every finding", but no second review cycle exists in `docs/reviews/`. No decision row records the build's
merge: E-288 records only the runs. The same commit edits `extended/three_way_baseline.py`, a pinned dependency named
at `136762d` in H4's Appendix C and section 18. The edit adds `walked_mirror`, an `isfinite` guard and the "N-1" scalar
walk `_walk_one`, described as "bit-for-bit identical" to `three_way_redraw.walk_outcome`. No row names the new commit
of that dependency, as section 16 item 1 requires ("every dependency of Appendix C merged at the commits the go row
names"). The model code (5,346 lines) was committed directly on `master`, not merged from a worktree.
*Mitigation:* I-1's sub-hash equality on P-14/P-15/P-16 is independent evidence that those three schedules are right
on this build. It says nothing about the firm-path code H4-B runs.
*Remedy:* a re-review of `31c507e` against the c1 findings, and a lead row accepting the build and its new dependency
commit, before any H4-B output is believed.

**F-2 (blocking for the naive coin; E-288). The nested-cut causality check (I-2) omits the naive coin.**
`h4_identities.csv` has I-2 rows for `p14`, `p15` and `p16` only. H4 section 4 declares the nested cuts as the check
of H4's causality through the cut ("the day blocks built on the cuts ending 2022-12-30, 2023-06-30 and 2023-09-29 must
each equal those built on the cut ending 2023-12-29"), with no exception for the coin. The coin is the one baseline
with no registered sub-hash (I-1). I-5 checks its source only in P-16's mode: registered marks, latency 0, P-16's
sides, void calendar off. So the coin's run-mode schedule (L = 5, the clock grid, the void calendar, its own sigma)
has no causality identity at all.

**F-3 (blocking until diagnosed; E-288). P-15 and P-16 each lose 310 trades to the sigma rule, far beyond its declared
scope, and the schedule actually used is unhashed.**
In `h4_supply.csv` the `all` rows show `dropped_sigma_lt_2` 310 for both `p15` and `p16`. Their `trades` (180494 and
146421) fall short of the registered `search_trades` that I-1 compared (180804 and 146731) by exactly that count. H4
section 7 admits this drop only "where the store holds fewer bars behind an alert (the first minutes of its first
trading day only)". The naive coin, on the same five-minute grid, drops 1, as the declaration implies. The build review
(its line 59) expected 1 for P-15 and P-16 as well. The drop rebuilds the DayBlocks from the kept trades
(`scripts/h4_rebaseline.py:456-459`). Yet every identity was taken on the unfiltered schedule:
- I-1 and `schedules_sha256` hash the 180804- and 146731-trade sets;
- I-2's notes say "raw DayBlocks".

So the schedule H4-B consumes is covered by no identity and recorded by no hash. This also departs from section 5:
"equal to its registered trigger-level trades ... never trades pruned".
*Failure protocol, as far as I could take it:*
- Terminal state: trades removed from supply before any outcome.
- Stage: the H4-S schedule build, at the `CS.alert_sigma_cross_session` call (`scripts/h4_rebaseline.py:219`) on the
  registered builders' rows only.
- Component: `extended/h4_coin_source.py:80-140`.
- Replication: not run, because it needs the bars and an H4-B part holds the machine. Proposed for the lead under
  `run.lock`: the 310 rows' (date, decision) pairs, listed from the cut schedule (no seeds needed).

**F-4 (blocking for the sizing row; E-288). H4-0 did not measure what the declared cut rule needs.**
`h4_0_probe.csv` `rss_base_mb`, `rss_after_syn_mb` and `rss_after_real_mb` are blank. H4 section 11 says H4-0 measures
"the process's base memory", and Appendix A says "base 0.4 GB assumed (H4-0 measures it)". The probe gives one
synthetic cost a lane-trade (`us_per_lane_trade`) with no race/walk split. H4-S does not count "their mean R-10 steps"
or the news/max-hold time-limit shares (section 5): `h4_supply.csv` has only `max_flatten_share_across_R`. Appendix A's
cut rule ("H4-0's measured cost (the race's and the walks' terms at H4-S's time-limited share and mean steps) times
H4-S's counted supply") therefore cannot be computed from the files. E-288 states no memory fit.

**F-5 (blocking for H4-B's collection; E-288). The sizing's hours cannot be traced, and the live H4-B does not follow
the sizing.**
"~5-6.5 h/part" has no formula, and no input in a result file reproduces its upper end (see the table). It ignores
H4-S's supply: the same four ranges for P-14 (`trades_per_session` 26.6) and the naive coin (201.5), against the cut
rule's "times H4-S's counted supply". The parts that ran, from their logs (`stage2-extended-runs\h4-b-*.log`):
- P-14 ran in **five** unequal parts, 0..258, 259..452, 453..599, 600..769 and 770..1033. The part over 259..452 took
  9.11 h, above the 8 h cap (`PART_CAP_H`); 0..258 took 7.79 h.
- P-15 ran in five parts, 0..125, 126..293, 294..461, 462..629 and 630.. (live).

H4 Appendix A requires any split "in the lead's sizing row before it starts". E-288's own fallback reads "split

fields, so this is not proven.
