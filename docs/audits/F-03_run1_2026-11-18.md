# Audit Q-117a: `results/fo_opening.csv`, F-03's run 1, stages A and B (2026-11-18)

**Auditor:** independent. I re-derived the numbers from the CSV and its manifest with pandas, numpy, scipy and hashlib
(`docs/audits/scripts/f03_run1/rederive.py`, sha256 `b6804db4...`; its output is `rederive_output.txt` beside it). It
imports nothing from `extended/` or `vendor/`, runs no engine, reads no bar or walk. Three cross-checks read
vendored or repository **data** files as plain CSV: `vendor/stage2/results/u_value_map.csv`, the S1/S3 lift files, and

only to learn what each column means and which seeds and paths were used.

**Verdict: defects found. None of them moves the pick, and the closure by Kill B stands.** Three readings the
declaration promises are missing from the file: A8's realised risk, the Benjamini-Hochberg reading beside stage B, and
the Table 9 figure beside A9's MDL. A8's absence comes from a defect in the runner, which writes an empty column
without saying so. Every number that decides the outcome re-derives from the CSV, apart from the bootstrap quantities
SE_c and q_B (see "Not verified").

**Why the pick is None, from the CSV alone.** The reason is **Kill B (10.2): no eligible cell has L_c / SE_c > q_B.**
- It is not "every cell killed at stage A". Manifest `killed` is {}, the CSV has no `kind` = killed row, and all three
  cells carry stage B rows.
- It is not "a cell above q_B but ineligible". No cell is above q_B at all, so the eligibility conditions of 10.3 do
  not decide. They are reported, and they re-derive.
- s2 is the only eligible cell, and its ratio is about half of q_B.
- **How robust this is to the figures I cannot recompute:** for any max-T over approximately normal standardised
  draws, q_B is at least the one-cell 95th percentile, 1.645, and s2's ratio is below that floor. Overturning Kill B
  would need s2's SE_c overstated by a factor of about two (q_B divided by s2's ratio). The reported q_B lies inside
  the bounds for three cells under normality: 1.645 if fully correlated, 2.121 if independent.

## Findings, most serious first

1. **A8 (realised risk) is declared and absent. This is a runner defect: it writes an empty column without saying so.**
   - **Evidence:** the column `realised_to_intended_capped_risk` is in the manifest's `columns` list and empty on all
     2398 rows. F-03 section 9 A8 asks for "the openings' realised risk against intended in P-14's registered
     allowance-capped form ... a build check: the table's sizing is unchanged."
   - **Failure protocol (no rerun):**
     - *Terminal state:* the three `wait` rows with `design` = design carry no value.
     - *Stage decomposition:* the openings frame. `scripts/fo_opening.py` at 5c80dbb (lines 745-749) computes the
       ratio only `if "realised_risk" in ops`, and otherwise writes NaN.
     - *Component in isolation:* `extended/opening_wait.openings` at 5c80dbb (lines 469-470) keeps a fixed column list
       that does not include `realised_risk` or `intended_capped_risk`, so the condition is always false.
     - *Replication:* not needed, because the code path is deterministic and was read statically.
   - **Diagnosis:** a build defect, not noise. A8 decides nothing in stages A and B (it is neither a kill nor an
     eligibility condition of 10.3), so the pick is untouched. But the declared build check was never made, and the
     same code path feeds floor (ii) in run 2 (`floors["ii_realised_risk"]`), which a closed family never reaches.
2. **Benjamini-Hochberg at 10 %, declared "beside, never selecting" (10 item 1), is absent.** No column or row holds
   it, and no producing module computes it. *My approximation, exploratory and not the declared reading:* if L/SE is
   read as a normal statistic, BH at 10 % over the three cells rejects nothing, because the largest ratio, s2's, is
   below the smallest BH threshold (z of about 1.83). It cannot change the pick, since it never selects.
3. **The Table 9 figure "beside" A9's MDL is absent.** The rows exist in `results/calibration_power.csv` (manifest
   verified; `part` two_sample, `df_rule` treated, `form` placebo-selection, `method` cons, `k` 15, `value` net3,
   `share` 0.25): `difference80` is finite for `window` and `block5`, the deciding classes of s1, s2 and u3. It decides
   nothing.
4. **A1 item 2 (E-011's walker identity) leaves no positive record.** The runner (lines 1864-1870) refuses with
   `REFUSED (F-03): A1 item 2 ...` and exit 3 if `identity/walker_identity.py --synthetic` fails. The log has no such
   line and the run went on to `EXIT 0`, so it held, but neither the log nor the CSV says so. Items 1 and 3 are shown
   by the log's guard line and the manifest's `pinned_blobs` and `identity_rows`.
5. **The pick row does not name its rule.** `choice`, `kind` = pick, has `killed` True and an empty `reason`. Kill B
   is re-derivable, as above, but the file does not say it. The closing row should.
6. **Reporting granularity (A5).** `supply` `stratum_share` is keyed by (day kind, year). The declared stratum is
   (mark, day kind, year), and the per-session form of "in-state offers per session" is not written. This is a reading
   only.
7. **V_B's parts are not additive.** `V_B_in_wait` and `V_B_fallback` are each priced separately: the design's
   in-wait (fallback) openings against the twins' in-wait (fallback) openings (`fo_opening.py` lines 821-823). They do
   not sum to V_B's point value, and the file does not say so. They, and `V_B_p16`, are **point** dollar figures,
   reported beside as 10.5 requires. They must not be cited as money without the lower limit beside (Stage 2 D-145).
8. **Documentation discrepancy on k.** The brief says "k = 11". `CLAUDE.md` ("the next is k = 11") and `STATUS.md`

   The manifest's `k` 12 and `level` 0.05/156 follow E-095, which is correct. The two lead files are stale.
9. **Disclosed, not a defect: the judged block's calendar entered run 1.** The coin's grid of 1691 sessions is the

   These are dates only, with no value, bar or walk. Run 1's bar and walk reads carry no purpose, and
