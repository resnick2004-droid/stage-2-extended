## 3. Findings, most serious first

**C1. The identities do not cover the label.**

- Both declared identities held, but they cover only x3 at the day band's marks (against this repository's `extended.features`) and the day set (against A1).
- Nothing ties the following to Stage 2's walks or to this repository's walker, whose `identity/walker_identity.py` covers fixed-band walks without the breakeven move:
  - the label (`open_counts` on the excursions up and dn);
  - IV;
  - the entry-bar convention;
  - the frozen and overnight x3;
  - M, x1 and x2.
- feature-rules section 3 asks that a new labeller reproduce Stage 2's walks before it labels anything new.
- The open shares and the whole calibration rest on that label. It is supported by synthetic tests and the red team's mutants (all caught), not by an identity on real bars.
- I found no wrong number; this is a gap in what the identities cover.
- **Recommended before a T7 result binds a survey:** a light identity. Compare T7's open label at fixed point stops with the walker's (or A3's) flatten outcome on the same search-year marks.

**C2. Backward folds make decisions.**

- By the lead's ruling, written in the lever table and the docstring at 8d3d397 and so made before the run, each search-year mark is scored by its own year's fold model. 2020's marks are therefore admitted by a model fitted on 2021 to 2023.
- This is allowed: E-221 O-1 admits any internal split on the search years, and no feature reads the future.
- But it departs from F-R 6.3, a forward walk with test years 2021 to 2023 and no reverse walk.
- It also departs from the red team's H-n3 advice: "use forward folds for anything that decides".
- It was declared, so it is not exploratory-unlabelled. It should be stated beside any T7 result.

**C3. The manifest does not describe its diagnostics.**

- The manifest's `description` reads "coefficients, calibration and counts only", and `notes` is null.
- But 732 of the 973 rows are out-of-fold diagnostics: `oof` 156 + `reliability` 162 + `bracket` 414.
- The label "diagnostics, not a forward test" exists only in the module docstring, the lever table and the lead's draft row. The file and its manifest do not carry it.
- The loader uses none of these rows (per its docstring), so nothing decides on them today.

**C4. The manifest records no hash of its inputs.**

## 4. The licence question (E-179, E-245): no

- **No price unit anywhere.** Every value in the file is one of these:
  - a coefficient on log ratios of points² to points² (y, x1, x2, x3);
  - a dimensionless calibration value (alpha, beta);
  - a probability, share, loss or R²;
  - a count.
  No column holds a date, a clock time, a price, a stop in points, or M.
- **No price level is recoverable.** Every quantity is built from price differences, so adding any constant to every price changes nothing in the file.
- **No range in points is recoverable.**
  - Every stop is c·sqrt(M), in units of each window's own trailing move, and c is a fixed declared grid, not a fitted value.
  - Scaling every price by a constant changes the file only through the label's one-tick offset (tick / sqrt(M)). That offset is a small term pooled across hundreds of thousands of rows and folded into shares. It cannot be separated out, and it identifies no session's range or level, even with the public tick (0.25), contract and multiplier.
- **Contrast with the earlier files.** Q-121's file held ranges in points and in percent, which together give back the open price (E-179). S1's k values invert to a pooled quantile of the trailing range in points (E-245). Neither applies here.
- **What the counts reveal.** They show day counts, void days and how the anchors move with daylight saving. That is calendar information, not prices.
