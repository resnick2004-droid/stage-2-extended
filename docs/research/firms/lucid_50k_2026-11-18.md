# Lucid Trading, $50K accounts: every plan's evaluation and funded terms (Q-135b, E-196)

**The question.** For every account type Lucid Trading sells at $50K today, what are the current evaluation and
funded-account terms? Where do they differ from what this repository holds? And do the rules allow passing in one or
two big days ("flip days"), in the evaluation and up to the funded account's first payout?

**The answer.**
- **Plans on sale.** Lucid sells four plans at $50K on its own help centre: LucidFlex, LucidPro, LucidDaily (each an
  evaluation, then a sim-funded account) and LucidDirect (sim-funded straight away, with no evaluation; this is Lucid's
  "instant funding"). LucidMaxx is now invite-only, with "no public purchase option". LucidBlack is legacy.
- **LucidFlex.** Every term we model is unchanged: $3,000 target; $2,000 end-of-day trailing drawdown that locks at
  $50,100; 40 % evaluation consistency, or 50 % as a paid add-on; no consistency once funded; 5 days of at least $150
  per payout cycle; 50 % of profit up to $2,000 per request; 90 % split; at most 5 payouts, then a discretionary live
  review. One thing is new on record: breaking the evaluation consistency rule does not fail the account. The trader
  "would keep trading until your largest day is back within it."
- **Flip days.**
  - LucidPro's evaluation has no consistency rule and says "Pass the evaluation in one trading day". A one-day
    evaluation pass is allowed there.
  - LucidFlex needs at least 3 days at 40 % (2 with the add-on). LucidDaily needs 2 at 50 %.
  - No plan allows a one- or two-day path to the first payout, except LucidDaily's funded account. It has no
    consistency rule and no day count, only profit above a $52,100 buffer.
    - LucidFlex needs 5 days of at least $150.
    - LucidPro has a 40 % per-cycle consistency rule, so at least 3 days.
    - LucidDirect has a 20 % per-cycle consistency rule, so at least 5 days.
- **Prices.** No price for any plan is on the help centre. lucidtrading.com returned HTTP 403, so every price except
  Stage 2's Flex card prices of 2026-11-05 needs the lead's browser.

**Read date and method.** Every page below was read on **2026-11-18** (UTC; the machine clock showed 2026-11-17 HST).
Pages were read with one WebFetch GET each, with no login, form or account, and nothing was sent. The fetch tool
returns processed text and caps any single quote at 125 characters. Text in quotation marks came back marked as
verbatim. Text without quotation marks is the tool's paraphrase and is labelled "(paraphrase)". The rules-verifier
should re-read every row before use, as CLAUDE.md requires. A page date is the date the page shows ("Written by ...,
<date>"). "Updated yesterday" is copied as shown: relative to the read, it means about 2026-11-16/17.

**Article URLs.** `A:<id>` = `https://support.lucidtrading.com/en/articles/<id>`. The index is
`https://support.lucidtrading.com/en/`, and its ten collections are unchanged from Stage 2's 2026-10-30 reading.

## 6. Path to live (all plans; New Live Structure, A:16558826, 2026-10-14; A:16010520, 2026-10-16)

  Daily Profit for their LucidDaily account size"**; "If they have exceptional performance in sim funded"; "If they

  unchanged, so it was either added since or missed then.

  maximum payout level, not a guaranteed minimum for live eligibility." Unchanged.
- **What moves and what closes.** Funded accounts "with at least one payout" move; "All simulated prop accounts are
  closed when a trader is moved live, or opts out of trading live via cash-out."; "If one household member is trading
  live, others may not trade simulated accounts." Unchanged.
- **The $50K live account.** "$0 starting balance"; a $2,000 EOD drawdown; "No daily loss limit"; "4 minis or 40
  micros"; daily payouts; the MLL "locks at $100". Live bonus: $4,000 target, 20 days, $2,000 bonus. Cooldown: "2
  weeks". Unchanged.
- **Cash-out.** "equal to 50% of a traders live starting balance", with a 90/10 split; you move to live automatically
  if you do not choose within 24 hours. With a "$0 starting balance", the cash-out amount is unclear on the page.

## 9. Flip days: what the rules decide

The minimum number of profitable days is arithmetic on the stated cap c. The largest day must be at most c of the
profit, so at least ceil(1/c) days are needed: 2 at 50 %, 3 at 40 %, 4 at 30 %, 5 at 20 %. These are rules only; no
data is read.

| plan | evaluation: one or two big days? | funded: first payout in one or two days? |
|---|---|---|
| Flex | **No** at 40 % (3 days, "as few as 3 days"). **2 days** with the 50 % add-on (the firm's words, with a $1,560 cushion). A breach extends the evaluation; it does not fail it. No other rule against big days | **No.** 5 separate days of at least $150 per cycle (A:12945796). Whether DLL-on intraday payouts skip the 5-day gate is not stated |
| Pro | **Yes, one day:** "Pass the evaluation in one trading day"; no evaluation consistency | **No.** 40 % per-cycle consistency means at least 3 profitable days; plus $500 goal and $52,100 buffer |
| Daily | **2 days** at 50 % (consistency article); the 30/40 % options need 4 or 3 days | **Yes in principle:** no consistency, no day count, any day once the balance is at least $52,600 for a $500 request. With the default intraday trailing drawdown. A day of $8,000+ sends the account toward live |
| Direct | (no evaluation) | **No.** 20 % per-cycle consistency means at least 5 days; first-cycle goal $3,000 |

No page read forbids big-day passes by name. The only "flipping" rule (A:11404728) is about minimum-day trades, and it
is allowed. Integrity and microscalping rules apply as usual.
