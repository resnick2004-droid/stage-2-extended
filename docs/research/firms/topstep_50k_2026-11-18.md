# Topstep $50K: every account type sold now, evaluation and funded, read on Topstep's own pages (Q-135a, E-196)

Scout note for TASK_QUEUE Q-135 (scout a of a-d). Read-only. No account, login, form or purchase; no personal data
sent; no data read; no outcome computed. **Nothing here can be loaded by code.** A term becomes usable only when the

(CLAUDE.md, "Firm rules").

## The question

Which $50K accounts does Topstep sell now, and what are their evaluation and funded terms (price, targets and limits,
sizing, consistency, payouts, conduct)? What changed against our records, and do the rules allow passing the
evaluation, or reaching the first funded payout, in one or two big days ("flip days")?

## The answer

- **Topstep sells three $50K evaluation products now.**
  - **The standard Trading Combine**, on two price paths: Standard ($49 a month plus $149 activation) or No Activation
    Fee ($95 a month, $0 activation). Its terms are unchanged against Stage 2's ledger. Every value re-read today
    matches.
  - **Two Topstep Labs one-time-fee Combines, which are not in any of our records.**
    - #006, the **$50K Static Drawdown Combine** (October 2026): $149; a $4,000 target; a static $2,000 MLL; a
      mandatory $1,000 DLL; 4 minis.
    - #010/#011, the **$50K Daily Payouts+ Combine**, in an EOD version and a Static version (November 2026). Its
      funded account has **no consistency rule** and a **Daily payout path**: after a $2,000 buffer, $250 to $1,500 a
      trading day.
- **No instant- or direct-funded $50K product was found** on the official pages. The help centre's article index
  lists none, and the topstep.com home page shows no products. This is UNVERIFIED rather than confirmed absent, so it
  is on the list for the lead's browser.
- **Flip days.**
  - **Evaluation.** The evaluation cannot be passed in one day, and Topstep says so: "Can I pass the Trading Combine
    in one day?" / "No." It can be passed in two days, if the best day is at most 55% of the total profit. A larger
    best day does not fail the account: it raises the target to best day / 0.55, and the rest must be earned on
    another day.
  - **Funded account.** The Standard path's first payout needs 5 winning days of at least $150 and has no consistency
    rule, so one big day plus four $150 days is within the published payout rules. The Consistency path needs 3
    trading days with the best day at most 40% of net profit.
  - **Labs Daily Payouts+ funded account.** It has no consistency rule at all, and one payout of up to $1,500 is
    allowed each trading day once the $2,000 buffer is cleared.
  - **Conduct limits on big days.** No page sets a numeric rule against big days. Three texts bear on them:
    - "Account Stacking" ("run high-risk attempts until a large win occurs") is a named prohibited strategy;
    - Terms of Use s.8 says sizes "substantially" larger than your other trades fall short of market-standard risk
      management;
    - the RTP triggers include "You max position a majority of your trades".

## Flip days: the facts that decide them

### Evaluation (standard Combine, either price path)

- **One day: not possible, and stated.**
  - TCP (v): "Can I pass the Trading Combine in one day?" / "No. Big spike days don't build funded traders,
    consistency does."
  - Mechanically, a one-day pass has best day = total profit = 100% of total, above 55%.
- **Two days: allowed by the published rule.**
  - CON (v): "You can pass in as few as 2 days, as long as no single day exceeds 55% of your total profit and your
    Profit Target is met."
  - Example: $1,650 then $1,350 passes.
  - A best day over 55% does not fail the account. The target becomes best day / 0.55; CON's example is $1,800 →
    $3,273.
  - The rest "must be earned on a separate trading day". A $3,000 first day therefore needs about $2,455 more, on
    days none larger than the first.
  - The engine's `max(target, best day / share)` reading (RD-01 section 4) is this rule.
- **No minimum-days rule and no time limit** (TCP, SUB). Stage 2's `combine_min_trading_days` = `none_stated`
  already records the same FAQ.
- **Labs Combines.**
  - #006 Static and #011 Static: target $4,000, so the best day is at most $2,200 for a two-day pass.
  - #010 EOD: target $3,000, so at most $1,650.
  - Minimum days: ABSENT. One-day FAQ: ABSENT.
  - The Labs DLL is a mandatory $1,000, and the Static versions cap NQ at 4 minis (#006). A single trade's loss is
    therefore bounded per day. The day's profit is not.

### What limits big days without a number

These are tier-1 and tier-3 matters (Stage 2 D-145). Each is a reading, not a ruling: the conduct tier is the
operator's (E-007 item 4).

- PTS "Account Stacking": "run high-risk attempts until a large win occurs". This reaches a strategy that buys many
  evaluations and swings for a flip in each.
- ToU s.8: sizes "substantially" larger than your other trades fall short of market-standard risk management.
- PC: "is not a viable strategy" catch-all.
- RTP triggers: "You max position a majority of your trades"; "Multiple accounts hit the Maximum Loss Limit in one
  day".
- TCP: "Big spike days don't build funded traders".
- **No page states a numeric cap on a single day's profit outside the consistency rules.**
